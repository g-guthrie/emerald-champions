# Economy review — approved decisions and implementation, September 10

The user paused the sequential campaign audit to settle the whole economy. They approved paid specialists with early access/full Lilycove stock; all three 100/250/500 soot milestones; replacing Bottle Cap drops with treasure; converting the powder vendor to cash; retaining Coins/BP/garden berries; PP upgrades surviving set changes; and Glalitite/pearl Shoal rewards. The implementation and scoped native evidence are recorded below. Historical candidate explanations follow; they are not evidence of full-game economic playtesting.

## Concrete proposed direction

Keep free moves, nature/ability/Stat Point editing, competitive held gear and fixed 31 IVs. Money pays for finite field supplies, capture supplies, evolution purchases and decoration. Eliminate free-item resale before judging prices. Free preset-generated held items and berries must be included, not just the Center shop inventory. Do not change base item prices globally to zero: those values also serve other item mechanics; implement explicit sale eligibility using the actual free-supply definitions.

Evolution stones/tools: remove ordinary evolution items from the unlimited free archive; retain legendary form tools there. Sell a small early selection through a specialist before Roxanne, expand specialist selection as the player travels, and put the complete collection in Lilycove. Retain meaningful world gifts and shard trades. Suggested starting prices: ordinary stones 3,000; uncommon consumable tools 5,000; reusable devices 10,000 purchased once. These are design candidates, not a measured campaign budget. Starting cash is 3,000; mandatory reward totals and supply budgets still require measurement before prices are final. Do not make every early evolution wait until Lilycove.

Soot: lifetime 100 = reusable Linking Cord; 250 = Marshadow unlock in Route 113 ash grass; 500 = Houndoominite moved from Fiery Path. Existing soot balance remains spendable on glass goods, independently of milestones. Claim through the glassmaker, show current balance, prices, total collection and next milestone. Milestone gifts need full-storage recovery. Keep flute utility and optional furniture, but reduce 6,000/8,000 furniture grind in the eventual price pass. Route 113 contains 270 ash tiles, so these targets are based on map scale, not timed player traversals. Marshadow remains uncaught until captured, with normal quest encounter rules and existing Sweet Scent behavior, no selection menu; sign/Fallarbor guide reflect progress and capture. Exact rate must be stated when implemented (current quest standard is 1%, native ungated residents 3%).

Distribution: remove Houndoominite's existing Fiery Path pickup if moved; do not remove Flannery's Cameruptite. Remove Marshadow from the Circuit reward source, and explicitly maintain the timing of every other existing Circuit reward. Currently that timing is calculated from list position, so changing a source without adjusting the schedule silently advances later rewards. Recheck Eternatus's completion requirement and all Circuit/Center dialogue. These are pending implementation details, not completed changes.

Lilycove: 1F reception/lottery/directory; 2F everyday medicine and basic capture supplies; 3F advanced balls and expedition supplies; 4F evolution stones on one counter, special evolution devices on the other; 5F existing decorations; roof drinks and clearance decorations. Keep clerks' stocks complementary. No paid moves or free competitive-gear duplicates. The actual 4F currently has two tutor-referral clerks and no merchandise.

Bottle Caps: retire acquisition as a player-facing reward, replacing the Pickup entry with ordinary treasure of an appropriate cash value. Do not add Hyper Training for already-perfect IVs. Dormant item IDs need not be renumbered or deleted. Current Bottle Caps are sellable treasure, not a functioning separate currency.

BP: retain Circuit earnings and the supply/decoration counters. Paid evolution tools become an optional BP alternative only if no longer free at Centers; otherwise replace that shelf. Keep BP useful for finite supplies and special balls; no compulsory BP grind for basic team editing. Price BP shelves relative to actual repeatable awards, not inherited Battle Frontier assumptions.

Coins: retain the optional Game Corner and its coin-purchase alternative. Current cash equivalents: starters 10,000 each, Poipole 130,000, Genesect 150,000. Those legendary prices need a campaign cash-budget decision after closing free resale; preserve present distribution until that decision. No new requirement to win slots.

Garden berries: retain the three existing one-time Mega trades, 20 eligible garden berries each. Keep their currency separate from freely generated battle berries. No need for an additional Bottle Cap or powder exchange layer.

Heart Scales: retain the actual one-for-one PP Up exchange, but fix preparation wiping paid PP upgrades before treating this as a satisfying reward. Proposed behavior is that investment survives battle-set changes; precise move/slot persistence requires implementation review. PP Max and PP Up are overlapping denominations of one upgrade, not separate currencies.

Berry Powder: retire Slateport's solo campaign dependency on the link-only Berry Crush minigame; convert the vendor to a cash herbal/PP-supply specialist with selected stock. Do not invent a new farming currency merely to keep the old jar. Retain garden berry uses for planting, contests and the Mega exchanges.

Shoal materials: current reward is already a reusable Linking Cord, not a Shell Bell despite old script labels. A cord soot milestone would make this particularly redundant. Proposed replacement: a first completed collection awards Glalitite moved from Seaspray Cave, with later material trades paying valuable pearls. Confirm the species/evolution/access timing and both tide entrances before approving that exact relocation. Do not charge materials for an item already freely supplied.

Other small sinks: retain useful ordinary cash fees (Safari, museum, Day Care, drinks, decorations) without adding new currencies. Fossils remain exploration/revival objects. Lottery/Trainer Hill prizes, held treasure, Pay Day, Pickup, repeatable rematch income and BP wagers need integration checks against the final prices. This draft does not claim every such branch was runtime tested.
