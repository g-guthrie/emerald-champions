OFFICIAL FIELD GUIDE

Petalburg Woods

Move Needed: HM01 (Cut) for the eastern half
The woods join the northern and southern halves of Route 104, and they are the first place this game shows its hand. Four Mega Stones lie in here, more than any other early map, and the wild list is twice the length of the route outside. Bring Poké Balls and a second Pokémon: the Aqua grunt in the middle of the wood fights a double battle.

Maps and Events
OFFICIAL FIELD GUIDE

Event 1: The Devon Researcher

He is out here looking for Shroomish, of all things

Step onto the clearing north of the southern entrance and a Devon researcher stops you. He is carrying survey records back to Rustboro and would rather talk about Shroomish. Let him finish.

Event 2: Team Aqua Takes the Papers

The grunt was waiting to ambush him
The researcher puts you between himself and Aqua

An Aqua grunt walks in behind the conversation and demands the Devon papers. The researcher retreats behind you, which is how this game introduces its factions: you are in the middle of it before you have a second badge.

This is a double battle. The game checks your party before the scene starts and will not run it with a single Pokémon, so catch something in the grass first. Beat the grunt and the researcher hands over a Great Ball on his way out.

NOTE
None of the three starters learns Cut, so the eastern third of these woods, and the Scolipite in it, stays shut until you catch something that can. A Shroomish from this very map will do.

Event 3: Cut Opens the Deep Wood

Two cuttable trees wall off the eastern third

Come back with HM01 and the trees at the middle of the map come down, opening the path east to the deep wood and the Scolipite beyond it. The same passage drops you back onto a corner of Route 104 you could not otherwise reach.

TIP
Rock Smash pulls Pineco, Dwebble, Crustle, Ferroseed and Heracross out of the boulders here, at levels 10 to 16 while the grass is still handing you level 6. It is the fastest way to get a real Pokémon before Rustboro.
