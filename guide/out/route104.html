Maps and Events
OFFICIAL FIELD GUIDE

Event 1: Visit Mr. Briney's House

The old sailor, and the boat you will need later

A cottage sits on the southern shore with a boat tied beside it. Mr. Briney is the only way across to Dewford, and he will not sail for you yet. Knock anyway, so the cottage is on your map when Devon sends you back here with a package.

Event 2: The Flower Shop and the Leaf Stone

The florist outside gives a Leaf Stone for nothing

North of the woods, a shop covered in petals. The women inside are tending plants rather than selling them, and one of them hands over the Wailmer Pail for watering berries. The florist standing outside gives away a Leaf Stone, which is a free evolution this early.

Come back once you have a Secret Base and the shop opens properly, selling the six decorations listed opposite.

TIP
Take the balls, not just the stones. This one route hides a Love Ball, a Friend Ball, a Dive Ball and two Net Balls. Specialty balls are scarce for a long stretch after this, and the Net Ball in particular is the answer to everything living in Petalburg Woods next door.

Event 3: Gina and Mia, the First Double on the Road

There is no way round them on the bridge
They battle as a pair, and so do their Pokémon

The bridge on the northern half is held by twins who fight together. Their four Pokémon are built as two pairs: Plusle and Minun feeding each other through Plus and Minus, then Volbeat and Illumise, both with Prankster, so their support moves go first whatever your Speed says.

Your first two Pokémon are the two that come out, so order your party before you step on the bridge. Bring a Ground type if you have one: it turns half their team off.

CAUTION
Volbeat's Tail Glow raises Special Attack by three stages at once, and Prankster Roost puts the health straight back. If you let it set up twice you are not winning this with chip damage. Taunt it, or knock it out.
