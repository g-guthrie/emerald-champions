# Open decisions for the owner (Sept 22, 2026)

Each item is a design call; the code works either way and nothing is half-done.

1. **PokéNav story calls.** Match Call is removed and Wally's "now I can call
   you anytime" call is gone. Five scripted one-off calls remain: Dad on
   Briney's first sail (every save), Roxanne 250 steps after Badge 2, Scott
   after Winona, Scott after the postgame Cosmog scene, and the rival about
   Rayquaza. Keep them as story beats, or remove them (one-line each)?
2. **Rare Candy gifts at former TM spots.** The ledger converted some to
   sellables (Nugget/Big Pearl/Star Piece) as redundant with the free Leveler.
   About 21 still give a Rare Candy (Route 104, Rustboro Mart, Dewford Hall,
   Steven, Museum, Fan Club, Cycling Road ×2, Trick House ×3, Mauville,
   Verdanturf Mart/Center/Tent, Lavaridge, Route 111, Fossil Maniac…).
   Converting all adds roughly ¥80k to the economy. Convert, keep, or mix?
3. **Gabby & Ty.** Their battle re-arms every time you enter Routes 111, 118
   or 120 — an endless paid rematch, while routine rematches are retired.
   Make it one-time (interviews can continue) or keep?
4. **Norman's Mega Stones.** He always gives Sceptilite, Blazikenite and
   Swampertite, whatever starter region you picked. Match the chosen region?
5. **Ranking Hall and "Single results" boards** still show Singles records,
   which can no longer grow. Switch them to Doubles records?
6. **Champions Circuit desk and Game Corner Genesect / Starter Archive** lost
   their entry in the Inclement rebase (code kept; the Lottery now uses a
   daily draw). Restore entries, or retire the code?
7. **Held for earlier:** Incinerate/Regenerator policy, whether in-battle
   Restart should roll back the Bag, Poké Vial spending a charge at full HP
   and Hyper Training (both match Inclement).
8. **AI replacement choice after a KO (doubles).** The planner never picks
   replacements after a knockout or forced switch — its decision clock has
   expired — so the controller sends out the first healthy party slot. This
   predates the integration; all battles were tuned with it. Fix (smarter
   replacements, harder battles) or keep?
9. **Elmer (Ashen Woods)** says he shows off Bug types; his authored team has
   none. Change the line or the team?
10. **Wallace's Diancie-room exhibition.** A one-time post-League battle with a
    different team. The design says post-League Wally is the only rematch-style
    fight — keep Wallace's exhibition as intended?
11. **Frontier difficulty.** Facility trainers now use the campaign doubles AI
    profile (they previously had a minimal one, or none in early Factory/Tent
    runs). Keep, or tone down early facility streaks?
