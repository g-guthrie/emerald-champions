# Approved wild-area revision — September 8, 2026

A subsequent [wild-area follow-up](WILD_AREA_POLISH_2026_09_08.md) improves Route123 and adds optional Woods Rock Smash Crustle. The tables and build evidence below record the preceding 108-area implementation.

Implemented all 108 area recommendations from the [personal review](WILD_AREA_RATINGS_2026_09_08.md), with the user's subsequent correction: **Pineco, Heracross and Ferroseed are available in first-visit Petalburg Woods grass. Rock Smash is supplemental.** The 30 areas rated 10 retain their complete encounter tables, including the three Dive habitats, Dewford Meadow, Verdanturf Meadow, the gem chamber and Seafloor's relic room.

The pass changes **469 species slots across 135 native tables in 108 maps**. Individual rod tiers account for 148 changed methods. Native levels, encounter frequency, slot weights, map/method access, alternate Altering Cave populations and inactive regions are unchanged. This pass makes no species-stat, trainer, cap, battle-mechanic, map-layout or item-access changes.

The opening now includes Route101 Mienfoo 13%, Bonsly 12% and Eevee 8%; Route102 keeps Timburr 11% and Pachirisu 8%; Route104 adds Croagunk 8% and Roselia 10%. First Woods grass contains Heracross 11%, Pineco 8%, Ferroseed 5%, Shroomish 13%, Slakoth 12% and Pichu 8%, among its other residents. Woods2 supplies Parasect 13% and Beedrill 11%. Its monkeys, Applin and Larvesta remain; Woods3 retains Kartana, Goomy, Phantump and the existing useful dark/ghost/fighting choices.

Field-method timing informed the relocations. The opening forest tools use grass, Dewford Mantyke keeps its Old Rod, Route106 adds Old Rod Remoraid, and Route110 keeps Chinchou 40% while adding Tadbulb 60% to the Old Rod already obtainable in Dewford. Minccino remains on Route117. Later rods deliberately offer stronger returns to earlier areas.

There are **652 direct ordinary Hoenn species/form identifiers**, compared with 641 before this pass. Every previous evolutionary family still has an ordinary catch. Golbat, Gurdurr, Kakuna, Machoke, Metapod, Mightyena, Nuzleaf, Pupitar and Skiploom now come through retained earlier evolution lines. Paras comes through breeding the wild Parasect: configured Paras evolves to Parasect, Parasect is breedable, and the existing Daycare resolves the egg species by walking backward through evolution targets. This is source review of acquisition mechanics, not a completed breeding playtest. Regional and special forms retain direct encounters, including all Pumpkaboo sizes, three-member Maushold, both Rockruff types, Antique Sinistea and Artisan Poltchageist.

Land/Rock Smash remain authored in [native encounter data](../src/data/wild_encounters.json). All 58 water maps retain exact species lists in the [water authoring sheet](../data/emerald_champions/wild_route_sheet.json); generation makes zero further changes. The [after roster](../work/wild-approved-20260908/after_area_rosters.json) records every one of the 138 areas in native slot order. The [slot diff](../work/wild-approved-20260908/slot_changes.json) records every actual before/after species change.
