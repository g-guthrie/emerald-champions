# Personal review of every ordinary wild area — September 8, 2026

I individually read all 138 active Hoenn area rosters after the habitat revision and stronger early-choice changes, including every available land, Surf, rod and Rock Smash method. These scores are my design opinions about the actual Pokémon, their encounter chances, their surroundings and when a player can use them. They are not computed variety scores or results from playing every area.

A 10 means I would keep that area's current distribution for this demanding campaign. It does not mean every catch must be fully evolved. Early powerful discoveries and useful babies are part of the intended game. A later room can also deserve a 10 without an exclusive species if its common population makes the visit worthwhile.

There are 30 areas I would keep and 108 where I would make a specific improvement. This includes criticism of my own recent changes, especially Patrat on Route123 and fish on Seafloor's walking floors.

Scope: one row per registered active Hoenn encounter map, using the default population. Eight alternate populations of the same original Altering Cave are not eight additional areas. Landmark-only legendary rooms do not have ordinary distributions to rate. Origin's three legacy `UNUSED_RUBY_SAPPHIRE_MAP` IDs are connected side chambers and are included; those source IDs do not mean they are inaccessible. Shoal's five rows represent its registered encounter populations across its tide layouts.

**Implementation status:** The user approved these recommendations and the [area revision](WILD_AREA_REVISION_2026_09_08.md) now applies them. The scores below describe the pre-change roster. The later user correction requires Pineco, Heracross and Ferroseed in first-visit Woods grass; that supersedes any wording below suggesting exclusive Rock Smash access.

**What I would change to reach my 10/10**

Keep the 30 areas rated 10 as they are. For the others, these are my preferred changes. Exact replacements and concentrations describe the intended result; a subsequent implementation still needs to allocate every slot, preserve forms and family availability, and check access against progression. Do not delay Mienfoo, Timburr, Pachirisu, the early babies, Pheromosa or Kartana, and do not weaken trainers or gyms. Keep the three actual Dive rosters unchanged.
