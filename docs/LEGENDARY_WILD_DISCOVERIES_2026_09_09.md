# Legendary wild discoveries — September 9, 2026

The 34 encounter-stone actors are removed. Their 54 species now appear in 34 habitats as rare wild encounters, distributed throughout the campaign. Shaymin is available before Roxanne; nothing in this distribution requires becoming Champion. The weather disturbance brings rare Pokémon out of isolation and into suitable habitats. NPCs describe sightings, research and practical requirements; Devon provides an optional, revisitable guide.

## Encounter rules

- Each eligible, uncaught local discovery gets its own 1% outcome in the standard wild selector. These outcomes replace ordinary residents. Existing special roaming/outbreak checks precede standard encounters.
- Sweet Scent uses a 25% total rare-legendary roll, then chooses evenly among eligible, uncaught local discoveries. With two eligible discoveries that means 12.5% each; with three, about 8.33% each. Ordinary Ultra Beasts remain in their existing wild tables.
- Sweet Scent respects badges, story events and NPC requirements. It does not need a Devon visit.
- Failed or fled wild encounters remain available immediately; a capture permanently removes that discovery from the eligible pool. Already-caught one-time ordinary residents yield their slot to another resident instead of cancelling an ordinary encounter.
- Rare discoveries are generated at the current player level cap with an existing non-Mega battle preset when available.
- Surf discoveries use the water encounter method; Seafloor Cavern seabed discoveries use the existing underwater encounter method. The new Cave of Origin B1F table gives Terapagos a working habitat.

## Local quests and clues

Meloetta and Landorus use the existing saved discovery bits for permanent quest completion. Bringing the required partner and speaking to the correct local NPC unlocks encounters; leaving the partner in the PC afterward is allowed. Neither Pokémon nor items are consumed. Route 102 introduces Shaymin through an existing NPC; Route 117 introduces Type: Null through the flower gardener. Existing route information boards and several naturalists show the same live local availability guide used by Devon.

The script rewrite removes the old Sign/network story from Steven, Devon, Magma, Aqua, Mt. Pyre and the weather crisis. Local hints describe current habitats and gates. Ordinary road signs, evolution stones, Mega Stones, and the original legendary set pieces retain their appropriate meanings.

## Existing discoveries during the campaign

- Lilycove ferry expeditions and the Champions Circuit open with the Feather Badge (six badges). The sailor supplies the S.S. Ticket and four island passes, and introduces the Latias/Latios roaming choice. The other twin remains at Southern Island. Mew, Deoxys, Lugia and Ho-Oh keep their original island encounters.
- Groudon and Kyogre weather reports become available after resolving the Sootopolis crisis. Rayquaza retains its summit encounter after the crisis.
- Mewtwo and Pecharunt unlock with the Mind Badge; Pecharunt still requires Okidogi, Munkidori and Fezandipiti. Magearna remains an eight-badge Devon discovery requiring Diancie.
- Arceus is a Devon reward after the weather crisis and eight badges, before the Elite Four. It no longer requires completing all other legendary discoveries.
- Existing Circuit, Game Corner, breeding, Regi puzzles, visible legendary encounters and ordinary Ultra Beast habitats remain distinct acquisition methods. Their rewards can be pursued during the campaign.

These tests do not execute complete map traversal, dialogue choreography or a full capture battle.

The wild-table checker passed for 265 active tables. The final named release build completed, and all current release gates passed: compiled map/tile integrity, current authored/generated agreement, wild-table integrity, input/artifact identity, ROM header/checksum and GBA memory bounds. The release is `pokeemerald-legendary-wild-20260909-release.gba`; its `.inputs.json` binds 28,979 build inputs to the ROM/ELF bytes. This is build and targeted native-function evidence, not a completed fresh-save campaign playtest.
