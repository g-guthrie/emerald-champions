# September 8 battle AI repair evidence

This is a focused native repair record, not a certificate of campaign difficulty
or complete battle-mechanic coverage. The September 5.1 design book is not the
acceptance criterion.

## Runtime changes

The paired chooser no longer replays the full battle engine for every move pair
and switch/Mega combination. It caches the native damage/accuracy and independent
move opinions once per board, then evaluates four ordered actions using small
local HP, survival-probability, Speed, field and support state. The ordinary board
reuses the turn-initialization cache. Modified switch/Mega boards refresh it.

Every legal move/foe target remains available. Healthy positions only investigate
purposeful phase changes; pressured positions also rank legal reserves. Voluntary
switching shortlists one ability-aware reserve per flank, with native evaluation
still deciding whether it is worth giving up an action. Forced replacement ranking
retains all legal reserves. Weather/terrain setters, Palafin, Regenerator,
Natural Cure, lock/immunity problems and authored partner-tactic pairs can open
switch candidates without waiting for critical HP. This is a deliberate search
tradeoff, not a claim that the best possible reserve is always retained.

The forecast explicitly handles Helping Hand, Follow Me/Rage Powder versus
single-target attacks, After You, damaging Instruct, normal/side protection,
Trick Room and Tailwind order, immediate rain/sun damage and weather Speed/accuracy,
Justified, Weakness Policy and Steam Engine activation, absorption boosts/healing,
direct partner healing, Sash/Sturdy survival and inaccurate-KO retaliation risk.
Charged moves cannot masquerade as immediate attacks without their native charge
exception. Hidden Commander actors do not generate fictitious attacks or targets.
Ally activation has no flat Justified/Weakness Policy/Steam Engine bonus: the
resulting damage, action order and survival must justify its friendly-fire cost.

The scoped Perish policy recognizes the native internal-zero death deadline,
including an actor that gained Soundproof after the song was applied. A genuine
simultaneous deadline permits two distinct reserve switches; ordinary turns do
not gain an unbounded double-switch search. An authored singer can pivot one
turn earlier while its Shadow Tag partner still holds affected opponents.

Native move opinions and compiled trainer plans remain inputs, not unconditional
commands. Duplicate field setting is rejected. Trick Room is allowed to expire
and be established again, rather than spending both partner actions on two
toggles that cancel when either actor is interrupted. Utility preferences require
a relevant field/stat benefit. Campaign Prediction and contradictory Risky,
Highest Damage and Force Setup traits were removed by the trainer-data repair.

## State and stack defects

The native transaction fixture exposed two omitted scratch values in the switch
snapshot: `gPotentialItemEffectBattler` and `gBattleMovePower`. Both are now restored.
The existing 1 KiB fixture coroutine stack also overflowed into its own probability
bookkeeping during a direct AI evaluation. It now matches the 4 KiB native battle
stack allowance. The transaction case checks a stack-edge canary in addition to
board, AI cache, field, items and both RNG state invariants. Passing this fixture
does not prove the maximum stack depth of every unrelated mechanic.

## Measured latency

The full-party fixture uses six Pokemon on each side, a legal active Mega,
multiple Mega-capable reserves, spread attacks and support options. Measurements
use emulated GBA VBlank frames, not host wall time. One frame is about 16.7 ms.

| Measured path | Frames |
| --- | ---: |
| First cached forecast before reserve/cache refinements: paired selection, cold | 168 |
| Same early implementation: paired selection, subsequent turn | 101 |
| Refined forecast: complete turn initialization plus cold paired selection | 6 + 39 = 45 |
| Refined forecast: complete turn initialization plus subsequent paired selection | 6 + 16 = 22 |
| Same refined subsequent position called directly from the EWRAM fixture | 18 |
| Small two-Pokemon-side fixtures, initialization plus selection | usually 3–5 |

These are specific observed positions, not an exhaustive worst-case guarantee.
The cold full-party case is still about 0.75 seconds; subsequent selection plus
initialization is about 0.37 seconds. The historical repository log reported
515/374 paired-decision frames for 5.1, but that is historical evidence, not a
freshly rebuilt same-session baseline. Temporary per-stage logging was removed
from production source after measurement. The bounded timing fixture remains.

The common expert declarations passed after the probability/healing fixes:
state restoration, accurate guaranteed KO, reciprocal Helping Hand and Justified,
single-owner Mega, Follow Me protecting a Room setter, Room expiry without
self-cancellation, weather establishment without repeated setting, direct healing
preserving a slower winning attack, and the full-party timing probe.
The native compiled plan/owner lookup passed. The generic weather and terrain
group passed all 8 weather and 8 terrain parameter cases.

Two inherited Sandstorm/Shore Up parameters were removed: they demanded weather
for a full-HP partner that cannot benefit from healing. No bonus was added to
satisfy that invalid contract. A separate inherited non-campaign-profile
After You/Trick Room test passes its Room case but fails its Moonblast negative
expectation; it does not invoke the common paired chooser and is not claimed fixed.

Actual authored trainer-party tests are maintained in
`test/battle/ai/emerald_champions_plans.c`. They use the production compiled catalog
and native party/stat generation, not invented opponent moves or mocked plan bits.
Observed passes include Flannery's After You/Eruption, Connie's mutual Storm Drain
Surf, Parker's Room/Instruct Earthquake against Focus Sash targets and the unsafe
non-Telepathy partner control, and Tabitha's survivable Surf/Steam Engine/Weakness
Policy activation plus the injured-recipient safety and already-sufficient
unboosted-damage controls. Maura's authored three/four-turn Perish sequence passes:
the singer pivots early, the trapper holds the affected foes, then escapes its own
deadline while the original opponents faint. Tests were adjusted
where the original opponents made another move genuinely sensible: Tyranitar's
4x Fighting weakness favored Hammer Arm over Earthquake, and Sand Stream plus
Assault Vest made Connie's modest extra Surf damage worth less than Protect.
Those changes did not alter authored trainer teams or add artificial move bonuses.
The final Connie fixture puts paralysis on both opposing Tyranitar, so Gastrodon
already outruns them and Tailwind has no speed-crossing payoff. The third Tabitha
fixture checks the native minimum-damage cache to prove unboosted Heat Wave is
already enough; its initial higher-level targets did not satisfy that premise.

The authored tests uncovered a namespace mistake: ordinary recorded battles
retain the real campaign trainer IDs. Rejecting every `RECORDED` battle silently
disabled their policies in the native harness. The resolver now rejects recorded
**link** and other foreign namespaces, not ordinary recorded campaign battles.
Positive and negative lookup cases pass, and the final authored run consumes the
actual compiled plans without a test-only policy mock.

The committed anonymous four-turn Perish countdown preserves both original
opponents. A separate direct chooser assertion on that initialized native board
sets simultaneous zero deadlines and checks distinct escape choices. The direct
Perish-policy guard fixture also passes. This separates actual committed countdown
evidence from an intentionally constructed decision-state assertion.

The narrow Quincy Gas-exit change is eligibility-only: an authored SUPPRESS actor
can offer a non-Gas, non-dependent reserve when its live Truant partner has usable
Entrainment and a native-legal recipient after suppression ends. No other Gas may
remain. Temporary ability-cache queries restore their values immediately. Actual
switch scoring remains authoritative. This case was reviewed and compiled but
was not independently played as a committed Quincy multi-turn sequence.

## Remaining boundaries

This is a bounded one-turn forecast, not a second complete battle engine. In
particular, dynamic terrain changes, delayed Wish, drain/recoil, Counter/Mirror
Coat and complex secondary/multi-turn effects are not fully replayed for every
candidate; they retain native independent scoring where available. Accuracy and
survival use an expectation approximation, not a complete probability tree.
Passing the terrain opener choices does not establish exact same-turn terrain
damage/priority simulation. A few authored native tactics are not proof that all
trainers, ability interactions or difficulty settings are competitively strong.
