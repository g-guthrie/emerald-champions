# Emerald Champions Frontier Brain anchor designs

Seven Brains complete; 42 distinct species and 6 unique Mega slots.

## ANABEL

- Question: Can the player read which mind state is active and stop the lake trio, Psychic Terrain, Trick Room, and Mega Latias from handing control forward?
- First-loss lesson: Identify whether Anabel is playing fast knowledge or slow intuition, then remove the one controller.
- Strongest: The lake-trio narrative makes six Psychic legends feel like distinct mind states, not a mono-type pile.
- Team:
  - `SPECIES_UXIE` — `ITEM_LIGHT_CLAY`; `MOVE_REFLECT`, `MOVE_LIGHT_SCREEN`, `MOVE_THUNDER_WAVE`, `MOVE_PSYCHIC`
