# Individual authoring: E0001–E0009

September 8, 2026. Fourteen trainer branches, nine encounter records, 56 party
slots. Ratings describe the intended design after individual source review;
they are not measured win rates or a claim every encounter was played. All 56
members' moves were checked against their actual generated preparation arrays
and abilities against configured species/form data. No pinned move-pool veto,
campaign Item Clause or ordinary EV/4 calculation was used.

Native generation uses perfect IVs and flat Stat Points before nature. The
opening live cap is 14; Easy/Normal/Hard floor these negative-offset parties to
10/12/14. Party sizes, offsets and progression are unchanged. Native move effects,
actual AI conditions and preparation access—not plan/crack prose—are authority.

Changes: Treecko trades Quick Attack for Protect around Swords Dance; Mudkip
gets Liquidation/Superpower/Rock Slide/Protect instead of redundant Normal/
Fighting/Water coverage without protection. Torchic becomes Timid special
Speed Boost with Flamethrower/Heat Wave/Will-O-Wisp/Protect rather than a frail
physical recoil/setup set. Their offensive/Speed investment and Life Orb make
actual attacks worthwhile; Pikachu's Light Ball special damage, Fake Out and
Electroweb buy conditional windows instead of mandatory support every turn.

Retained reserves: bulky Eviolite Shroomish supplies legal Spore/Leech Seed/
Giga Drain/Protect pressure. Toxic Orb Guts Taillow supplies its own activation,
physical pressure and priority. Ground immunity, powder immunity, Fake Out
denial, spread pressure and opponent-specific coverage remain useful counters;
the player can prepare/catch a larger party before the rival. Repeated Taillow
is a rival identity here, reduced in other nearby teams instead of banned.

Actual dynamic path: battle_setup.c replaces the unchosen Hoenn starter with
the selected generation's matching stage, and ApplyEmeraldChampionsRegionalRivalSet
applies the current default/GetOpeningStarterSet result. Same-species Hoenn
branches retain the canonical loadouts above. Opening rival-only changes remove
unsupported sun assumptions from Bulbasaur (Overgrow/Protect) and Charmander
(Blaze/Flamethrower), add Protect to Rowlet, and replace Sobble's redundant
Water Pulse with legal Mud Shot. Player starter presets are unchanged.

## E0002 — TRAINER_CALVIN_1 — 8.5/10

Historical source-only review below. The executable-AI restart superseded the
Taillow reserve and parity-only berry explanation; see ai_restart_0002.md and
the current compiled party for the repaired Hoothoot version, now rated 9/10.

Poochyena gains Taunt over Play Rough: it can deny support while its partner
finds a survivable Drum, or attack/Helping Hand an actual attack. Zigzagoon's
Speed investment becomes HP/Attack because Extreme Speed already supplies the
closing speed advantage. Its 62/66/70 maximum HP at initial Easy/Normal/Hard
is even, so Belly Drum activates Sitrus; Gluttony is not what triggers Sitrus.
Both birds retain independent Guts/Reckless physical pressure, Starly retaining
U-turn. Ghost/redirection, Taunt/Fake Out, focused damage and Intimidate provide
distinct counters; burning Guts is not one. Below 10: the two bird reserves
overlap; a more independent special/defensive closing choice could deepen the
team without making the Drum opening harder by stat inflation.

## E0003 — TRAINER_RICK — 8/10

Historical source-only review below. The executable-AI restart superseded the
physical Dewpider and repaired Thread's paired Speed forecast; see
ai_restart_0003.md for current native evidence and the revised 9/10 rating.

Dwebble reallocates HP to 24 Speed points: Spinarak's 50/51/53 Speed precedes

an independent second control route could create a richer recovery phase.

## E0004 — TRAINER_ALLEN — 8/10

Historical source review; superseded by the executable-AI restart in
`ai_restart_0004.md` (8.5/10, current team changes and native evidence).

Bunnelby gains reliable Strength over Protect, keeping priority, Rock coverage
and U-turn; Huge Power gets a real repeatable Normal attack. Pikachu/Paras

## E0005 — TRAINER_TIANA — 8.5/10

Historical source-only review below. The executable-AI restart adds Gleam,
repairs drain/Sing/guard-Encore valuation and observes actual lead/reserve
decisions; see ai_restart_0005.md for the current9/10 rating and limitations.

Replaced another Guts Taillow with the first campaign Buneary: Klutz/Flame Orb,
fast physical investment, Fake Out/Switcheroo/Encore/Drain Punch. It gives a
different reserve disruption plan, not a forced Orb transfer into Fire/Guts.
Skitty's Sash/HP/Speed, Fake Out and Helping Hand support real special Swirlix
attacks; Sing remains a risky alternative. Sitrus is an actual condition for
Unburden, not automatic speed. Bulky Adaptability Eevee keeps physical reserve
damage plus Wish/Protect; Wish heals its position, not the other active partner.
Below 10: the opening and Orb endgame could cooperate more directly; a carefully
chosen switch sequence could make the disabled physical attacker a setup/recovery
window instead of merely removing damage.

Demonstrated engine fix: effective-item data hides Flame Orb under Klutz, but
the transferred item still burns its recipient. Trick/Switcheroo viability now
reads the offered item while preserving the effective hold cache and rejects
the burn bonus for immune/status-benefiting targets. Existing native AI file
contains one focused three-case regression: vulnerable physical Snorlax receives
Orb and burns; Guts Rattata and Fire Torkoal cause an attack instead. All three
cases passed in the named native test. This is mechanic-level proof, not a full
Tiana playthrough or a promise Switcheroo beats every other legal move.

## E0006 — TRAINER_BILLY — 9/10

Restart supersedes this historical source-only assessment: see
`ai_restart_0006.md` for implemented weather, Imposter and Population Bomb AI
repairs, 39 executed native parameters, retained-team tradeoffs and limitations.

Timid Lotad now precedes deliberately slower Modest Wingull: 50/51/53 versus
43/47/51 Speed. Wingull's transferred points support HP and special damage.
Rain can therefore improve Hurricane on the same turn, and Swift Swim benefits
Lotad after activation. Wide Lens Tandemaus and Scarf Imposter Ditto supply

more than the rain team; a weather-linked reserve could improve the transition
while trading away the unusually flexible Imposter answer.
