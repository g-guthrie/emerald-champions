## Authored decisions

- Establish: retain Choice Band Rock Head Adamant Sudowoodo252HP/252Atk/4Def,
  Head Smash/Wood Hammer/Sucker Punch/Low Kick. Native raw stats
  103/101/83/27/50/30 (HP/Atk/Def/SpA/SpD/Speed). Recoil-free direct pressure
  is the centerpiece, not a compulsory setup turn. It differs from the earlier
  Life Orb/Room-oriented and later Sitrus Sudowoodo sets.
- Support: Pumpkaboo changes Rocky Helmet to Eviolite, retaining Frisk,
  Impish252HP/252Def/4SpD and Leech Seed/Will-O-Wisp/Shadow Sneak/Protect.
  Raw91/50/77/34/44/42; effective defenses115/66. This trades contact chip for
  surviving support turns and distinguishes the nearby Helmet repeats.
- Fallback: Skiddo changes Sitrus to Eviolite, keeping Sap Sipper, Adamant
  252HP/252Atk/4Def and Horn Leech/Bulk Up/Milk Drink/Protect. Raw
  101/73/40/43/45/42; effective defenses60/67. It retains healing attacks and
  can exploit Grass immunity plus setup. Do not force a switch simply because
  an immunity is available when Sudowoodo can survive and remove the threat.
- Lilligant changes Mental Herb/Modest to Sitrus/Calm, with
  48HP/208SpA/252Speed. Protect becomes Pollen Puff. Native89/42/55/89/60/81
  versus original86/42/55/101/55/81: preserved Speed, more HP/SpD, less immediate
  special damage. Quiver Dance supplies later damage and121Speed; Giga Drain
  sustains it, Pollen Puff heals partners or attacks. The small HP investment
  complements Sitrus and setup survival; no automatic berry-parity rewrite.

The existing SETUP policy and shared bounded evaluator suffice. No shared AI

The initially described “empty double-Protect” was investigated in actual code:
`PairWaitingHasPayoff` permits waiting out a living foe's first-turn Fake Out.
That baseline used this allowance, not a repeated idle-guard regression. No
global guard change was justified. The revised reserve candidate already
attacked before Pollen Puff replaced Protect. Useful protection for seeded
damage, a partner attack or setup remains intact.

Additional Ice Punch replaces the ineffective Fighting attack into Pumpkaboo:
it survives three turns at57/37/51HP while Head Smash brings Gurdurr to13HP.
The isolated reserve version demonstrates sleep support, then actual Ice damage;
at3 Skiddo95/Lilligant41 face Gurdurr100. Both are partial pressure checks,
not full Ice-matchup wins or proof that all Fire/Flying pressure is covered.

Exploratory fixed-turn fixtures sometimes requested a turn after battle end;
their horizons were corrected without changing game behavior. Temporary cases
are removed from the retained suite. No new permanent test or engine exception.
