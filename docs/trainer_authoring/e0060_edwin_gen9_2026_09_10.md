## Coaching card and final stats

Establish: the matched sun/moon pair supplies physical and special attacks;
do not spend turns on speed control when both already move first. Exploit:
Rock/Psychic coverage picks the threatening target; useful Icy Wind makes the
slower partner competitive, while a justified burn or Protect can preserve
an attack on the following turn. Fallback: Prankster Meowstic establishes the
relevant screen and then attacks, with Dazzling Gleam available into Dark.
Do not assume a reserve promotion is a natural switch or a guaranteed pairing.

## Native verification and outcomes

Temporary `work/edwin_gen9_probe.inc` uses compiled trainer construction,
native stat generation and unforced opponent choices. Player preparations
are legal-move checked and generated at level30 with IV31/EVs. The standard
physical reference is defensive Eviolite Eevee/Gurdurr; the special reference
is fast Xatu/bulky Pachirisu. Explicit surviving pairs are controls, not
claims that they necessarily enter together in ordinary play.

- Full physical lead: Psychic/Zen Headbutt removes Gurdurr on turn1.
  Immediate attacks finish Eevee turn3 at Lunatone86/Solrock81HP, with no
  opening guards or unnecessary Icy Wind. This baseline strength is retained.
- Full special lead: Power Gem removes Xatu while Solrock Protects turn1;
  Psychic/burn then Psychic/Rock Slide finish turn3 at76/103HP. The burn on
  specially attacking Pachi is less selective than ideal; residual damage
  contributes, but it is not evidence of reducing Thunderbolt's damage.
- Lunatone/Meowstic versus physical pressure: Reflect1, then paired Psychic
  attacks finish turn3 at76/94HP. Versus special pressure: Light Screen and
  Power Gem remove Xatu1; Icy Wind/Psychic2 makes Pachi71→47; both attack
  turn3 and finish at84/86HP. Screen PP and single Icy Wind use are asserted.
- Meowstic/Solrock versus physical pressure: Reflect1, Gleam/Zen Headbutt2
  removes Eevee, Psychic/Protect3 preserves Solrock while chipping Gurdurr,
  and Psychic/Zen Headbutt finishes4 at105/25HP. Versus special pressure:
  Light Screen/Rock Slide1 leaves Xatu7HP, Gleam/Zen Headbutt2 removes it,
  and Psychic/Rock Slide finishes3 at57/37HP. No repeated same screen.
- Full Fake Out/Grass pressure: Mienfoo flinches Lunatone but Solrock's Zen
  Headbutt removes Mienfoo1. Solrock protects at31HP while Lunatone attacks2;
  both finish Lombre3 at108/20HP. The planned Taunt never executes because
  its user is already fainted; this is not a successful-Taunt control.
- Separate actual-Taunt control: Sash Mienfoo/Pachi against promoted
  Lunatone/Meowstic. Fake Out1 flinches Lunatone while Meowstic attacks.
  Reflect2 precedes Mienfoo's executed Taunt; Lunatone's Icy Wind removes
  Mienfoo and lowers Pachi. Both use Psychic3, leaving Pachi29HP and the
  opponents70/105HP. Taunt's animation is asserted, not inferred from input.
- Dark/Ghost counterplay: full Sableye/Misdreavus pressure removes Solrock2,
  brings Meowstic in naturally, removes Lunatone3, and defeats Meowstic5.
  Reflect followed by Light Screen does not rescue that lost position.
  The promoted Lunatone/Meowstic control survives longer against the same
  pressure, removes Misdreavus4, and uses actual Dazzling Gleam into the
  remaining Dark Sableye5. Gleam's animation/PP are asserted; Sableye consumes
  its berry and survives at52HP, then wins. New coverage is executable, not
  a claim that this deliberately Psychic-heavy team now beats its counters.
