This review covers the initial Normal, one-badge fight, not a separate native
late-return/rematch run. Levels: Pachirisu20, Heracross/Conkeldurr21,

## Personal team review — retained sets

No accepted moves, species, items, abilities, levels or EV changes. The existing
team executes its main strategies well. A Cobalion Helping Hand candidate was
tested and withdrawn because it did not improve the observed decisions.
Coaching prose is updated to describe actual execution and counterplay; prose
is not executable AI or completion evidence on its own.

- Pachirisu: Sitrus/Volt Absorb/Impish,252/0/140/0/116/0. Follow Me handles
  dangerous faster single-target attacks; Super Fang and Helping Hand support
  direct pressure when redirection is unnecessary. Keep Nuzzle as an option.
  Actual72HP/50Def/53SpD supplies balanced defensive support. No EV parity edit.
- Heracross: Choice Band/Moxie/Adamant,4/252/0/0/0/252. Megahorn/Close Combat/
  Rock Slide/Knock Off offer immediate knockouts rather than another setup
  turn. Native60 Speed exceeds ordinary Natu56, but not Scarf Natu84; this
  changes whether Pachirisu should redirect. Choice lock remains counterplay.
- Conkeldurr: Flame Orb/Guts/Adamant,252/252/4/0/0/0. Facade/Drain Punch/Mach
  Punch/Knock Off retain damage, healing, priority and Psychic/Ghost coverage.
  Native30 Speed and limited special bulk justify support rather than more
  arbitrary Speed EVs. The batch proves burn activation and subsequent attacks;
  it does not require Facade when Drain Punch or Knock Off has better utility.
- Hariyama: Assault Vest/Thick Fat/Adamant,252/252/4/0/0/0. Close Combat/Fake
  Out/Bullet Punch/Knock Off support the slow partner and preserve Brawly's
  nostalgic signature. Native116HP and special bulk give it a different role
  from the faster, Choice-locked Heracross.
- Hawlucha: White Herb/Unburden/Jolly,4/252/0/0/0/252. Close Combat/Acrobatics/
  Swords Dance/Protect. Raw84 Speed becomes168 after Herb consumption, and
  itemless Acrobatics gains its native doubled power. Preserve supported
  setup flexibility rather than replacing every possible setup with healing.
- Cobalion: Leftovers/Justified/Timid,4/0/0/252/0/252. Aura Sphere/Flash Cannon/
  Quick Guard/Coaching. Native86 Speed precedes unactivated Hawlucha84; after
  Unburden it does not. Special attacks diversify an otherwise physical team;
  Justified is not falsely counted as a demonstrated physical boosting engine
  on this special set. Quick Guard remains an available tool, not a guarantee
  that this policy always anticipates Fake Out.

## Native evidence and limitations

Baseline `work/brawly-gen9-baseline.log` exposed an overlong Fairy fixture:
the two-player reference ended on turn2, so a requested third turn was invalid.
No game defect was inferred from that fixture error. Corrected and expanded
`work/brawly-gen9-motifs.log`:4 groups/23 cases. The Helping Hand experiment
in `work/brawly-gen9-support.log`:4/23, but unchanged tactical outcomes;
Quick Guard was restored. Final `work/brawly-gen9-final.log`:4 groups/25 cases,
with outcome assertions and faster Fairy counterplay.

- Ordinary physical lead: Super Fang/Close Combat removes Timburr on turn1
  and grants Heracross Moxie; further attacks finish Eevee on turn2. Both
  trainer Pokémon survive, Pachirisu44HP/Heracross71HP.
- Ordinary Psychic lead: Heracross removes Natu before its attack. Helping
  Hand/Knock Off finishes Pachirisu on turn2; both trainers' leads stay full HP.
  This line does not need an automatic opening Follow Me.
- Faster Flying pressure: Choice Scarf Natu acts before Heracross. Pachirisu
  uses Follow Me, receives the Flying attack and absorbs the opposing Electric
  follow-up; both trainer Pokémon finish the turn at full HP. Heracross removes
  Natu and earns Moxie; Helping Hand/Knock Off finishes the remaining opponent.
  Follow Me is cast once, then replaced by useful offensive support.
- Faster Fairy spread: Scarf/Timid Ralts acts before Heracross. Dazzling Gleam
  hits through Follow Me, leaving Heracross47HP despite Pachirisu's redirection.
  Heracross removes Ralts, and next-turn support finishes the other opponent.
  The older slow Ralts reference was removed before using it as spread-execution
  evidence: Heracross had knocked it out before it could use Dazzling Gleam.
- Opening Fake Out stuns Heracross. Pachirisu still uses Super Fang; ordinary
  attacks earn Moxie and finish on turn3. This is recovery from disruption,
  not a claim that Follow Me can intercept faster-priority Fake Out.
- Middle reserve snapshots: against Psychic pressure, Hariyama's Fake Out
  prevents Natu's action while Conkeldurr removes it with Knock Off. Flame Orb
  burn activates, and subsequent Drain Punch/Knock Off finish on turn2. The
  physical reference also finishes on turn2 with both reserves surviving.
- Final physical snapshot: Cobalion Coaches before Hawlucha's Close Combat,
  which removes Eevee and consumes White Herb. Hawlucha reaches168 Speed with
  +1 Attack and restored Defense; itemless Acrobatics finishes Timburr on turn2.
  Cobalion's old last-move value is not proof of a second executed Coaching:
  Hawlucha can finish before its slower partner acts.
- Final Fake Out snapshot: Cobalion chooses Coaching, not Quick Guard.
  Hawlucha flinches and falls to29HP but retains the boosts. Another Coaching
  precedes Close Combat on turn2, activating Herb/Unburden and removing
  Pachirisu; boosted Close Combat finishes Mienfoo on turn3. Both survive.
  Successful Quick Guard prevention is explicitly not claimed for this test.
- Final Psychic/Electric snapshot: Close Combat/Flash Cannon nearly remove
  Natu; Protect/Aura Sphere finish it on turn2. Hawlucha attacks but falls to
  Electric pressure on turn3. Cobalion finishes Pachirisu on turn4, surviving
  75HP. Helping Hand in Quick Guard's slot did not improve this sequence and
  was withdrawn. This remains a useful preservation/priority-support backlog
  item, not grounds for another global evaluator branch.
- Complete sampled decision: setup6 + both actors29 =35 GBA frames (~0.58s),
  warm29. Six actual trainer members and six current player presets, including
  normal switch/form consideration. Not campaign-wide worst-case timing.
