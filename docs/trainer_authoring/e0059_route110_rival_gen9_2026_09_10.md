Establish: Flame Orb enables Swellow's Guts while Lightning Rod Manectric
protects its Electric weakness. Attack immediately when appropriate; an opening
guard that earns burn is different from an empty repeated guard. Exploit:
Facade/Brave Bird pressure and useful Manectric coverage/Snarl. Breloom's Toxic
Orb establishes Poison Heal; Spore and priority support surviving partners.
Fallback: Nuzleaf Fake Out/Pickpocket, Numel special coverage, or Lombre Fake

without assuming every Grass, Fire or Water replacement has the same role.

Breloom core returns in Route119, which has not received completion credit
from this review. Nuzleaf's Pickpocket and Numel's Oblivious coverage distinguish

## Hoenn native execution

All six aliases run both pressure boards and three pair contexts. Pair0 is the
actual full five-member party. Pair1 explicitly promotes companion/closer;
pair2 explicitly promotes Breloom/closer, with other members fainted. These
survivor controls are not claims of naturally chosen replacement order.
Actual phazing/replacements are tested separately. Player sets are native
level30 with checked preparation-legal moves; opponent choices are unforced.

- Shared physical opening: both guard turn1 while Swellow becomes burned.
  Facade/Thunderbolt removes Eevee turn2; Brave Bird/Thunderbolt finishes
  Gurdurr turn3 at Swellow45HP/Manectric36HP. No second consecutive guard.
- Shared Psychic/Electric opening: Swellow guards for burn while Manectric
  removes Xatu. Pachi's Thunderbolt aimed at Swellow is absorbed by Manectric,
  which reaches+1 then+2 SpA; the entry-stage assertion verifies the shield.
  Facade/Snarl follow-through finishes turn3 at78/19HP. A displayed last Snarl
  on the final turn is not an additional cast: Facade can end the battle first.
- Nuzleaf/Grovyle physical: Fake Out protects Grovyle while Nuzleaf survives
  Double-Edge, consumes Sitrus and steals Eevee's Eviolite. Both held items
  and the player's lost item are asserted. Grovyle ends41HP turn2 instead of
  the old13HP. Continued attacks leave Grovyle1HP/Nuzleaf36HP versus Gurdurr89HP
  after turn3, not a completed win.
- Nuzleaf/Grovyle special: new Eviolite enables Fake Out/Leaf Blade first;
  Sucker Punch/Quick Attack removes Xatu turn2 with Grovyle still81HP, versus
  the old12HP. Nuzleaf attacks while Grovyle guards turn3, leaving Pachi70HP.
  This is improved positioning, not proof of optimal finishing aggression.
- Breloom/Grovyle physical: guard for Toxic Orb, Spore/Leaf Blade, then Mach
  Punch/Leaf Blade. Eevee falls and Breloom heals47→57HP while Grovyle remains
  full; Gurdurr remains119HP after healing its earlier damage. Under focused
  special pressure Grovyle still falls turn3 despite Eviolite; Grass overlap
  has not been designed away.
- Numel/Combusken physical: Combusken attacks and gains Speed, but falls
  turn2; Numel falls turn3 to Gurdurr. Special reference is its better matchup:
  Heat Wave/Protect first, then coverage plus Fire Punch removes Xatu turn2
  and finishes Pachi turn3 at Numel101HP/Combusken72HP. With the rejected
  Eviolite, Xatu survived turn2 at3HP and Combusken dropped to2HP, versus the
  retained Life Orb's Xatu knockout and80HP Combusken at that endpoint.
- Breloom/Combusken physical: Spore supports immediate Fire Punch, then
  priority/Seed Bomb and repeated attacks while Speed rises. After turn3
  Breloom80HP/Combusken13HP face Gurdurr50HP. Special reference protects for
  Orb/Speed once, then attacks; Spore and Fire Punch remove Xatu turn3 while
  Pachi is asleep at67HP. Neither line waits indefinitely for more Speed.
- Lombre/Marshtomp: Fake Out supports Waterfall against physical pressure or
  Ice Punch against Xatu. After three turns, physical reference has Eevee
  fainted/Gurdurr78HP versus Lombre27/Marshtomp70; special reference has Xatu
  fainted/Pachi56HP versus97/25. Both retain useful attacks; these endpoints
  are not full wins.
- Breloom/Marshtomp: Spore and Waterfall, followed by Mach Punch or Seed Bomb,
  leave one sleeping foe after three turns. Physical endpoint57/70HP versus
  Gurdurr91; special83/25 versus Pachi43. Poison Heal provides recovery and
  toxic status prevents ordinary sleep on Breloom; no duplicate Spore into
  an already sleeping target is claimed from the logs.

Fake Out/phazing control uses Mienfoo/Dreepy against the actual full party.
Swellow removes Mienfoo before Dreepy forces it out; each actual starter enters
beside Manectric. Grass then gets phazed into Nuzleaf; Manectric finishes Dreepy
turn3 at Nuzleaf88/Manectric74HP. Fire uses Rock Slide/Snarl after Combusken's
entry and finishes turn3 at72/74. Water uses Ice Punch/Snarl and finishes turn2
at113/74. The turn2 Taunt user is already fainted: this is phazing/Fake Out
evidence, not successful Taunt disruption. Breloom's promoted scenarios above
are separate from these observed natural entries.
