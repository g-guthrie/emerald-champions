at enemy Timburr, but allied Lileep redirected it, Timburr survived and Binacle
fell. That was NOT deliberate ally support. Current source inspection shows
the pair forecast resolves Follow Me and credits explicitly targeted allied
absorbers, but does not generally retarget attacks into the attacker's own
Storm Drain partner. `IsDamageMoveUnusable` checks the defender's partner,
not every possible allied redirector. This is a shared forecast limitation,
not a broken native Storm Drain mechanic. The local moveset correction removes
the demonstrated conflict; it does not claim to fix all such teams globally.
