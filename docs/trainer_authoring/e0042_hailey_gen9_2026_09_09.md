Route109's event starts a standalone double battle with `TRAINER_HAILEY`.
First access is before Brawly: Dewford's boat requires the delivered Steven
letter, not badge2, because the player must reach Slateport to find Brawly.
`GetCurrentLevelCap` returns20 with one badge; the actual Normal constructor
and difficulty adjustment produce four level18 opponents. Player references
are native level20, IV31, with legal preparation moves. The catalogue's
placement after Brawly is review order, not proof of mandatory play order.
This is first-visit evidence, not a late-return/all-difficulty certification.

## Accepted team and executable strategy

- Move Wartortle into the lead beside Flaaffy; Marill becomes the first reserve.
  Previously the supported Shell Smash attacker was separated from its screen
  setter. Native review now shows Reflect/Protect, one White Herb Shell Smash,
  and subsequent boosted attacks. No fixed opening command is forced.
- Evolve the authored Tympole into Palpitoad, retaining Eviolite, Water Absorb,
  Modest,252HP/252SpA/4Def and its original four moves. Its Ground typing gives
  the three-Water roster an Electric immunity and a useful actual switch.
  There were no other Palpitoad authored slots; later Seismitoad teams remain.
  This preserves the beach/tadpole identity without replacing the team with
  another common redirection core. Wild/tutor configurations are unchanged.
- Retain Flaaffy's Light Clay/Static/Bold balanced bulk, both screens,
  Electroweb and Thunderbolt. It can screen or attack a vulnerable target.
  Retain Wartortle's White Herb/Torrent/Modest maximum special offense and
  Speed, Hydro Pump/Ice Beam/Protect, and Marill's Sitrus/Huge Power/Adamant
  bulk/Attack with Aqua Jet/Play Rough/Brick Break/Protect.
- A Tympole Helping Hand-for-Sludge Bomb candidate did not improve the observed
  support. It was withdrawn. Palpitoad retains Sludge Bomb for Grass coverage;
  coverage does not remove the need to respect faster Grass attackers.
- No accepted EV, ability, item, move, authored-level or shared-AI changes.
  Existing `EC_BATTLE_PLAN_SETUP` and native pair/switch evaluation execute
  the revised team. No new search, allocation or permanent battle test.
  Universal IV31, exact EVs and berry thresholds remain untouched.

## Native evidence

Final `work/hailey-gen9-final-verified.log`:5 groups/36 parameter cases.
Source fixtures are archived in `work/hailey_gen9_probe.inc` and
`work/hailey_complete_timing_probe.inc`, removed from the retained test build.

- Physical lead: Reflect/Protect on turn1, Thunderbolt/Shell Smash on turn2.
  Herb is consumed, both defenses restored, SpA+2 and Speed84. Turns3–5 use
  Hydro Pump; Smash PP stays14. Eevee falls on turn4; Timburr has6HP after
  turn5, with both trainer leads alive. This sample is not a completed win.
- Physical opening with Protective Pads Eevee also establishes one Smash and
  attacks, removing Eevee on turn3. Pads prevents the initial contact
  paralysis; later Thunderbolt paralysis still occurs. It is not evidence
  for a wholly paralysis-free fight.
- Psychic/Electric lead: Thunderbolt/Protect, then Light Screen/Ice Beam
  removes Natu. Direct Hydro Pump follows rather than an unnecessary Smash.
  Reflect on turn3 against the remaining special attacker is overcautious;
  it is a new screen, not a repeated failed cast, and remains a tactical limit.
- Fake Out/Electric lead: Wartortle guards turn1, then the AI actually switches
  Palpitoad into the Electric attack on turn2 at full71HP. No trainer has
  fainted. This is a real full-party transition, not a promoted reserve mock.
  A further screen/guard on turn3 is conservative, not optimal offensive play.
- Direct Taunt blocks Flaaffy's screens. The team attacks instead, switches
  Palpitoad in, and removes Mienfoo on turn3. The old Tympole version lost
  Wartortle in the corresponding line; the final version has no trainer faint.
- Concentrated physical pressure causes a native Flaaffy-to-Marill switch,
  preserving Flaaffy rather than sacrificing it. Wartortle Smashes and the
  new pair's Hydro Pump/Play Rough removes Timburr on turn3.
- Brick Break removes Reflect on turn1. Flaaffy re-establishes it on turn2;
  the paralyzed breaker fails to act that turn. A timer-only diagnostic was
  insufficient: active side flags and unchanged Brick Break PP distinguish
  removal from a full-paralysis skip. Re-establishing the removed screen is
  not a duplicate active-screen bug.
- Promoted reserve pair, physical pressure: Ground/Brick Break pressure,
  then Play Rough and Aqua Jet finish both foes in three turns, Marill37HP/
  Palpitoad23HP. The earlier Marill/Tympole pair lost Marill on turn2 and
  had not finished Eevee by turn3.
- Promoted reserves, Fake Out/Electric: Palpitoad attacks through the Electric
  immunity while Marill guards, then Play Rough and priority finish both foes
  in three turns with both reserves at full HP. The old pair lost Tympole.
- Promoted reserves, Psychic/Electric: Palpitoad removes Pachirisu, but Natu
  remains16HP and Marill falls; Palpitoad is left7HP. This is not a win claim.
- Fast Treecko Grass pressure: Palpitoad initially protects while Marill
  attacks, but Giga Drain knocks it out on turn2, and Marill falls on turn3.
  The team has a meaningful exploitable weakness. The first slow Lotad
  reference was knocked out before attacking and was not Grass-hit proof.

## Rating

8.5/10, an intention-based judgment, not a measured win rate. More selective
late screens and stronger pressure after defensive switches would improve
Hailey. The main setup now executes, the reserve has a concrete defensive job,
and common disruption receives useful responses without enlarging shared AI.
