Universal IV31, exact EVs and berry convenience remain. Printed stats above
exclude Eviolite's in-combat multiplier. Native species data is authoritative.
Dragon Dance gives Shelgon127 Speed and +1 Attack; Shell Smash gives
Turtonator148 and +2 Special Attack. These are different payoff windows, not
an excuse to add redundant speed control.

Coaching: establish immediate Dragon/Ground/Rock pressure; protect a threatened
partner when the other has payoff. Exploit Helping Hand, and attack after useful
reserve boosts. Fall back to immediate damage under Fake Out/Taunt or lethal
pressure. Gabite/Pupitar/Shelgon each have one current canonical occurrence;
later Turtonator sets include another Smash user and bulky Body Press sustain.
Keeping these families preserves campaign variety. These complementary motifs
do not by themselves fulfill a new entry among the50 requested ability themes.

## Rating

8.5/10, intention-based. Stronger Water/Grass protection for the opening pair
and safer reserves against spread Fairy/Ground pressure would improve Wyatt.
The supported setup and offensive payoff execute; isolated counter losses
remain accepted tactical limitations rather than new global-code projects.
