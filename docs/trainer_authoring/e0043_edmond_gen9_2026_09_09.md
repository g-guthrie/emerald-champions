# E0043 Edmond — Gen 9 native review

Route109's event invokes `TRAINER_EDMOND` as a standalone double battle.
As established from the actual boat/cap code in the Hailey review, first access
is possible with one badge, cap20. The Normal native constructor produces all
four opponents at level18. References are native level20, IV31 and legal
preparation sets. This does not certify later return visits or all difficulties.

## Team review and accepted changes

Krabby: Life Orb → Eviolite. Staravia: Choice Scarf → Eviolite.
All four species, abilities, moves, authored levels and original EVs are retained.
The two item changes improve actual survival and follow-through. A Krabby
80HP/252Atk/132SpD/44Spe candidate with Life Orb was tested and withdrawn:
native Speed fell39→30 without resolving its dangerous matchups, and the
physical lead lost immediate offensive tempo. Do not materialize that spread.

Stats above precede item/ability multipliers. Mantyke keeps maximum special bulk
to establish support despite its Electric weakness. Krabby retains full Attack
and Speed: Tailwind reaches78, above the ordinary Natu56 reference. Eviolite
trades away Life Orb's damage multiplier but improves the pair's selected
Helping Hand payoff and survival. Liquidation/Rock Slide retain Sheer Force;
X-Scissor is coverage, not a Sheer Force-boosted move.

Staravia loses Scarf's75 Speed and now has50, or100 under Tailwind. This is a
real tradeoff, not an unchanged speed relationship: unboosted Natu56 and
Mienfoo53 are faster. Eviolite lets it survive a hit, and freedom from Choice
lock enables Brave Bird followed by Quick Attack or Double-Edge. Recoil still
makes low-HP attacks dangerous. Koffing's physical investment and Eviolite
support staying on the field to clear boosts; Levitate avoids suppressing
the allied offensive abilities, unlike Neutralizing Gas. Will-O-Wisp remains
available, but this batch does not claim an executed Will-O-Wisp sequence.

The four unevolved coastal Pokémon retain Edmond's sailor identity, distinct
from Hailey's screen/Smash team. Existing `EC_BATTLE_PLAN_TAILWIND` and native
pair/switch logic execute the revised sets; no shared-AI change or search
growth is required. All508-EV spreads, universal IV31, berry rules and shared
wild/tutor sets remain unchanged.

Coaching card: establish useful Tailwind while covering Krabby; spend the
advantage on Helping Hand/direct attacks; use available priority or switch to
the reserves when the lead is threatened. Expose a boosted target by removing
redirection, then Clear Smog. Updated plan/crack prose describes this evidence,
not a hard-coded sequence or independent executable policy.

## Native scenarios and tradeoffs

Final `work/edmond-gen9-final-verified.log`:4 groups/24 cases. Outcome
assertions cover the supported knockout, no Tailwind recast, native withdrawal,
reserve survival/finishes, and actual removal of two Bulk Ups.

- Psychic/Electric lead: Tailwind/Protect on turn1. On turn2 Mantyke uses
  Helping Hand and Krabby's Liquidation removes Natu before it damages Krabby;
  Krabby remains full44HP, with Speed78. Tailwind PP stays14. On turn3 the AI
  withdraws Krabby for Koffing without any trainer faint. The original Life Orb
  sequence instead left Natu1HP, lost Krabby, then lost Mantyke.
- Physical lead: Tailwind/Protect, then Helping Hand/Liquidation. Mantyke falls
  on turn2; the native replacement is Staravia. Tailwind-assisted Brave Bird/
  Liquidation finishes the reference on turn3 with Staravia30HP/Krabby28HP.
  The original Life Orb lead also finished in three turns, but retained Mantyke
  and lost Krabby instead. The accepted change is not better on every board.
- Fake Out followed by Taunt prevents Mantyke from establishing Tailwind.
  The team does not repeatedly attempt it: Tailwind PP remains15. Krabby and
  Staravia fall under concentrated Electric pressure; Scald removes Mienfoo
  with Koffing remaining. This is meaningful counterplay, not a claimed win.
- Opposing Tailwind: both sides establish it, so Natu112/Pachirisu98 remain
  faster than Krabby78. Edmond casts Tailwind only once, but loses Krabby and
  Staravia under this pressure. Further Scald attacks and native replacements
  occur; this three-turn sample does not prove recovery or a full win.
- Promoted physical reserves: Staravia survives turn1 at5HP, whereas the Scarf
  version fainted. A second Brave Bird deals more damage but recoil/pressure
  removes it. Koffing finishes Eevee on turn4, surviving11HP. Its turn2 Protect
  is conservative; the batch does not imply perfect guard selection.
- Promoted Psychic/Electric reserves: Staravia survives the first Psychic
  attack at7HP, then Quick Attack finishes Natu on turn2. Koffing attacks
  Pachirisu. Staravia falls after the next attack, but Koffing finishes on turn4
  at5HP. The Scarf pair lost both members by turn2 without a player knockout.
- Supported enemy setup: Follow Me covers two Timburr Bulk Ups while
  Sludge Bomb and Staravia attacks remove Pachirisu by turn2. Once redirection
  is gone, Koffing uses Clear Smog on turn3, resetting Timburr's Attack and
  Defense from+2 to neutral. Staravia falls to the boosted attacker before
  that reset; Koffing survives at full59HP. This proves reset after exposure,
  not prevention of all setup or interception of priority.

## Rating

8.5/10, intention-based rather than a measured win rate. Better recovery when
Tailwind is denied and safer low-HP Staravia decisions would improve Edmond.
The main Tailwind-to-attack and anti-setup roles now have concrete native
evidence; strong Electric pressure still meaningfully disrupts the team.
