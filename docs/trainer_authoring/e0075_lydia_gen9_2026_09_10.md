- Froakie retains Sash/Protean/Timid special-speed, Hydro Pump, Ice Beam,
  U-turn and Protect. Smoochum retains Expert Belt/Forewarn/Timid special-speed,
  Fake Out, Ice Beam, Psychic and Shadow Ball. A Sash Smoochum trial lost the
  opening Gurdurr knockout and cost Froakie; withdrawn in favor of Expert Belt.
- Magby: Life Orb→Eviolite. Retain Vital Spirit/Adamant physical-speed,
  Fire Punch, Mach Punch, Thunder Punch and Protect. Durable recoil-free
  attacks work with Friend Guard and Helping Hand; priority remains available.
- Happiny: Calm special bulk→Bold252HP252Def4SpD; Protect→Seismic Toss.
  Retain Eviolite/Friend Guard, Helping Hand, Charm and Light Screen. Fixed
  level damage supplies meaningful offense without attack investment and a
  usable move under Taunt. Ghost immunity remains a real limitation.
- Gible: Sitrus→Eviolite, Iron Head→High Horsepower. Retain Rough Skin/Jolly
  physical-speed, Dragon Claw, Rock Slide and Protect. Ground STAB is now
  available without hitting the partner; Ice remains major counterplay.
- Larvesta: Flare Blitz→Fire Punch, Wild Charge→Thunder Punch. Retain Choice
  Band/Flame Body/Adamant physical-speed, U-turn and Bug Bite. Give up peak
  recoil damage for sustained punches; Water/Rock pressure and Choice lock
  remain exploitable.

Keep the existing Conservative/shared policy; no gameplay AI code changed.
Coaching: use immediate coverage or worthwhile Fake Out, preserve a threatened
lead when a better reserve exists, support Magby with Friend Guard/Helping Hand
or a useful screen, and attack with Seismic Toss when support is unavailable.
Charm remains situational, not a forced first turn. Gible/Larvesta supply
recoil-free physical finishing after the special leads.

Current canonical distribution: Froakie/Happiny/Gible each appear here only;
Smoochum/Magby have one other earlier appearance, and Larvesta has two later
Eviolite appearances. Preserve the six-hatchling identity and this distinctive
Choice Band physical Larvesta rather than replacing babies with generic aces.
