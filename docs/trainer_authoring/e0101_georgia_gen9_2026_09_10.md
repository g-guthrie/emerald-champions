## Withdrawn proposal — important correction

The intermediate Healing Wish→Heal Pulse proposal did **not** demonstrate the
intended protected recovery. Native `moves_info.h` gives Heal Pulse no Protect
bypass, whereas Life Dew explicitly ignores Protect; the native pair evaluator
models that distinction and the Life Dew battle script heals both teammates.
My early proposal incorrectly paired Heal Pulse with a guarding recipient.
That was a team-design mistake, not a demonstrated shared engine defect.
Heal Pulse was withdrawn, not patched to violate the native mechanic.
Life Dew is already in Hattrem's native learnset; it is not an invented tutor
exception. It trades the larger single-target heal for self+partner recovery.
