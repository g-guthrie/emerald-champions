## Rating

8/10, intention-based. Better protection from fast Electric/Fighting pressure
and more decisive finishing turns would improve this Grunt. Its coherent
offensive opening and now sturdier chip reserves suit the first stage of the
Museum sequence without turning it into another boss or replacing its identity.
