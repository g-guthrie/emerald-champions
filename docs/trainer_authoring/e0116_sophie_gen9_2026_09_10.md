## Team and coaching card

- Establish: Forretress supplies physical bulk, optional Spikes and Sturdy/Red
  Card disruption when an eligible opposing reserve exists. Delphox pressures
  immediately; this is not a mandatory hazard opening.
- Exploit: Body Press uses Defense; slow Volt Switch can bring a useful partner
  beside Delphox. Parasect can redirect for Delphox, put a threat to sleep, or
  block a spread attack while its partner attacks. Sawsbuck exploits Sap Sipper
  and heals through Horn Leech; Parasect heals through Leech Life.
- Fallback: attack instead of stacking unnecessary support. Coba buys one
  reduced Flying hit, not immunity. Grass types, Overcoat and Safety Goggles
  bypass powder; single-target Fire bypasses Wide Guard. Dry Skin heals Water
  hits but increases Fire damage. No Mega is on this team.

Changes from the pre-review canonical team:

1. Forretress Brave → Relaxed. Same252 HP/252 Def/4 SpD, Speed42 retained;
   Attack93→85 and Defense147→161. This improves its primary Body Press and
   physical durability while sacrificing some Gyro Ball damage.
2. Sawsbuck Muscle Band/Serene Grace → Coba Berry/Sap Sipper. Its original
   moves had no Serene Grace secondary-effect payoff. Keep Jolly4 HP/252 Atk/
   252 Spe and all four moves. Sacrifice the general physical item bonus for
   a one-hit Flying buffer and a useful Grass immunity/Attack boost.
3. Parasect X-Scissor/Protect → Leech Life/Rage Powder. Native updated Leech
   Life has the same80 power, heals half damage and has10 rather than15 PP.
   Rage Powder adds partner protection alongside Wide Guard and Spore instead
   of duplicating self-only protection. Keep Sash/Dry Skin/Careful252 HP/4 Def/
   252 SpD. Redirection can still sacrifice Parasect and is not always optimal.
4. Delphox Life Orb/Blaze/Timid special-Speed set and all four moves retained.
