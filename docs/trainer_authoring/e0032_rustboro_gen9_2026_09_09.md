# E0032 Rustboro rival — current Gen 9 review

## Actual encounter and changes

Source: E0032 in `data/emerald_champions/emerald_champions_battle_teams.txt`,
Rustboro City's rival scripts, and `ApplyRegionalRivalStarter` in
`src/battle_setup.c`. The remaining starter determines the opponent, not the
misleading trainer-ID suffix: RUSTBORO_MUDKIP is the Grass closer,
RUSTBORO_TORCHIC the Water closer, and RUSTBORO_TREECKO the Fire closer.
May and Brendan each have all three branches. One badge, player reference
level20; actual trainer levels19/19/19/20 on Normal.

All six branches retain Taillow/Electrike leads and Shroomish plus the remaining
starter as reserves. Changes:

- Electrike: Volt Switch to Helping Hand. Preserve Lightning Rod, Focus Sash,
  Timid special/Speed investment, Thunderbolt, Thunder Wave and Protect.
  The native comparison exposed Volt Switch leaving before an Electric attack
  that then knocked out Taillow. Helping Hand offers partner damage without
  that move-induced loss of redirection; ordinary switching remains available.
- Shroomish: Bold 252HP/232Def/24Speed instead of 252HP/252Def/4SpD.
  Native Defense49 to48, Speed24 to25, with unchanged69HP/33SpD. Dartrix's
  Tailwind now takes it to50, above the reference Pachirisu49, not48 below it.
  Preserve Eviolite, Effect Spore, Spore/Leech Seed/Giga Drain/Protect.
- Grovyle and Combusken: Life Orb to Eviolite. Preserve exact EVs and moves;
  give the unevolved closers hit tolerance without recoil. Marshtomp retains
  Sitrus and its bulky physical spread. Taillow retains Flame Orb/Guts.
- Existing conditional SETUP preference replaces NONE. This rewards viable
  stat-raising opportunities, not a forced first-turn setup or repeated boosts.
  It did not demonstrate every regional setup move under the sampled pressure.

The regional constructor starts from each species' canonical Doubles default.
Remove its hidden duplicate-item substitution: the campaign has no Item Clause,
so a second Eviolite is valid and should not silently become an offensive item.
Preserve those defaults' exact Gen9 EVs. In the same trainer-only override owner,
Ivysaur uses Overgrow and Charmeleon Blaze instead of unsupported sun abilities;
Monferno replaces Thunder Punch with Fire Punch (STAB, no own-Lightning-Rod
conflict); Drizzile replaces Water Pulse with Ice Beam (Water Pledge retained).
Opening-stage overrides and player wild/tutor presets are unchanged. These
regional overrides also reach later recognized rival battles; that is not
individual verification of those later encounters.

Establish: Guts pressure with Lightning Rod cover, useful Helping Hand or speed
control, and Shroomish disruption. Exploit: boosted Facade or the actual closer's
offensive role; use a setup opportunity when safe and useful. Fallback: coverage,
manual positioning, Eviolite bulk and recovery. Do not assume every regional
closer plays like Hoenn's starter or that every reserve pair is equally strong.
