## Test-helper correction and first-Mega latency repair

treats as a fixed stat in TESTING. AuthoredOpponent previously set that bit,
so a Mega kept its base Speed while other stats and ability changed. The helper
now retains explicit starting-order registration but clears its Speed lock;
native form recalculation produces Mega Kangaskhan83 rather than77. This is
a test-only correction, not a gameplay stat-formula change. The scratch-local
prototype was removed after centralizing the fix in AuthoredOpponent.
