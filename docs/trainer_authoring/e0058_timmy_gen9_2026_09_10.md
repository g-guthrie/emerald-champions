option, not an assumed or scripted action. The updated plan/crack describes

Native `GetImposterTransformTarget` uses the diagonal opponent in doubles and
rejects a fainted, substituted, transformed, disguised or otherwise ineligible
target. `TransformBattlerData` preserves HP/item and assigns copied PP. The
manual Transform reference exposes a temporary Choice-lock limitation; native
`CancelerChoiceLock` clears a move absent from the copied list during move
resolution, later than move selection. No claim is made here that this timing
matches every external generation rule. Denied-Imposter fallback and smarter
redirection-aware support are explicit follow-ups, not newly solved features.
No actual Volt Switch, deliberate Intimidate recycling or copying an already
boosted sweeper was demonstrated. Poison Jab/Thunderbolt secondary outcomes
in the recorded runs are not guaranteed strategy requirements.

## Experiments and verification boundaries

Baseline `work/timmy-gen9-baseline.log`: three groups/22 cases. A combined
Focus Sash Heliolisk/Eviolite Herdier candidate, `timmy-gen9-durability.log`,
passed the same scenarios but weakened important pressure/reserve sequences;
it was withdrawn. It improved some survival endpoints, not overall strategy.
The accepted Helping Hand candidate is not conditional on keeping those items.

`timmy-gen9-helping-hand.log` had overlong fixtures after new turn2 wins;
horizons were corrected. `timmy-gen9-follow-through.log` exposed a logging
bug: GetMoveName received MOVE_UNAVAILABLE after Transform. The scratch logger
now renders that sentinel safely; game moves were not changed to satisfy it.
`timmy-gen9-final.log` then passed all29 cases. The final asserted run corrected
an unnecessary scene-order assumption: Dry Skin's popup precedes the spread
animation, so the check asserts the popup and actual HP rather than requiring
it after Surf's animation. All29 cases were rerun successfully.

Complete cold six-player-party sample60 GBA frames (1.00s):7 setup+53 both
actors; warm53 excludes setup. Same as baseline, inside the1.2s ceiling but
not strictly below the preferred one-second target. Not a worst-case guarantee.

when Imposter cannot activate would improve Timmy. The stronger lead partnership
