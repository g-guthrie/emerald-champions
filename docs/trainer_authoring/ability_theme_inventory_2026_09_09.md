## Evidence definitions

- `compiled tactic route`: the canonical branch contains a `tactic:` record connecting the actor, move, and recipient, and `render_plans(read_teams())` exactly matches the current generated `src/data/emerald_champions_battle_plans.h`. It is conditional planner input, not observed execution.
- `static native candidate`: the exact Ability and a useful holder/companion move, item, or partner coexist in the parsed branch.
- `configured ability presence only`: the exact, legal Ability is present, but this inventory found no requested build-around ingredient. The Ability may still be defensively useful.

Party coexistence never proves simultaneous field position, targeting, survival, switch order, or AI choice. Conflict flags are review prompts, not bugs. In the occurrence column, four exact examples are shown for compactness; the JSON contains the complete list.
