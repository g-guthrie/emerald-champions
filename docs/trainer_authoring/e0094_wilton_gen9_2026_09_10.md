## Changes and coaching card

Claydol trades Ice Beam for **Trick Room**, retaining Sitrus/Levitate/Quiet,
252HP/4Def/252SpA and Earth Power/Psychic/Protect. This supplies a second setter
without adding shared AI branches or a separate rules engine. It can establish
Room while Cofagrigus attacks, or take over when Cofagrigus is unavailable.
The trade costs Ice coverage; it does not improve every matchup.

Retain the other three sets:

- Cofagrigus: Mental Herb/Mummy/Bold,252HP/252Def/4SpD; Trick Room/Shadow Ball/
  Will-O-Wisp/Protect. Physical bulk, Normal/Fighting immunity and one Taunt cure
  support setup. Burn and Mummy remain useful options, not newly demonstrated
  automatic burn/contact sequences in these samples.
- Golett: Eviolite/Iron Fist/Brave,252HP/252Atk/4Def; Shadow Punch/Drain Punch/
  Ice Punch/Protect. All three punches receive its ability bonus; Drain Punch
  provides recovery when damaged. Keep the distinctive unevolved ruins partner
  rather than adding another generic fast attacker. Do not claim it learns Room.
- Steelix: Steelixite/Sturdy/Brave,252HP/252Atk/4Def; Heavy Slam/High Horsepower/
  Rock Slide/Protect. Starts in reserve, not as a mandatory Mega lead. The full
  physical sample switches it in while Cofagrigus sets Room, then transforms
  on the following turn. Mega raises Attack and bulk without changing Speed.
  Sturdy is lost on transformation; Sand Force supplies no automatic payoff
  because this team has no sand setter. Do not invent a sand synergy.

Establish one useful Room, exploit Ghost/Psychic/Ground and punch pressure,
then use the appropriate reserve or surviving setter. Existing TRICK_ROOM
