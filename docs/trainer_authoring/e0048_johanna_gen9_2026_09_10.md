Dwayne and Simon are separate battles; the common callback checks all three
defeat flags for the reward. Reviewed scope is first-access Normal, one badge,

## Accepted authoring

- Feebas: Ice Beam → Helping Hand. Keep Sash/Adaptability/Modest and its full
  special offense/Speed spread. Muddy Water remains its stronger STAB chip,
  Icy Wind retains Ice coverage and speed control, while priority Helping
  Hand can amplify a more capable partner instead of relying on base10 SpA.
- Qwilfish: Black Sludge → Payapa Berry. Keep original Adamant252HP/252Atk/
  4Def, Intimidate and moves. The one-hit Psychic buffer buys the demonstrated
  survival needed to exploit Feebas's Icy Wind and attack. Lose passive
  recovery; this does not solve the Water pair's Electric weakness.
- Budew/Sylveon, all four species, abilities, other moves, level offsets and
  party order remain. No accepted EV change or shared-AI branch. The existing
  native evaluator handles sleep, sound attacks, healing and support without
  a new trainer-specific score bonus. Updated prose describes the intent;
  it is not executable AI by itself.

## Candidate rejected

Qwilfish252HP/132Atk/124SpD with Black Sludge was tested alongside Helping
Hand. Its native stats became68/55/51/27/35/41, but the extra five SpD did not
prevent the Psychic knockout. It also left Timburr9HP after four physical
reserve turns instead of finishing it. The original EVs were restored and
Payapa accepted instead. Legal EV totals alone were not treated as improved
tuning. No broader stat-rule or automatic HP-parity change was made.
