Call; the rematch uses the same ID. Reviewed scope is first-access Normal,
one badge, native cap20 and all four opponents level18. Route109 is accessible
before Brawly. This is not a later-rematch certification. Player references
use native level20, IV31, authored EVs and verified preparation moves.

## Accepted authoring and coaching card

Cherubi's Heat Rock becomes Focus Sash. It survives the demonstrated physical
and fast Fire openings, establishes sun and contributes further attacks or
Helping Hand instead of dying immediately after setup. This trades eight-turn
sun for five-turn sun; the observed lead payoff fits within that duration.
The change does not guarantee survival against double targeting, multihit
attacks or residual damage.

Keep all four species, moves, abilities, original EVs, level offsets and party
order. Leafeon remains Life Orb; Luvdisc remains Focus Sash; Ponyta remains
Eviolite. No shared AI branch or new search is added. The existing executable
SUN plan rewards useful setup and rejects already-active weather; the revised
human-readable coaching card describes that behavior, rather than implementing
it by itself.

Establish: Cherubi sets useful sun, with Leafeon protecting when that preserves
its payoff. Exploit: Chlorophyll accelerates both Grass partners, Weather Ball
becomes Fire coverage, and Leafeon attacks. Restore sun after opposing weather
actually replaces it. Fallback: Ponyta supplies a Fire immunity and strong
physical coverage; faster Luvdisc can heal a damaged partner, attack with Fairy/
Ice moves unaffected by sun's Water penalty, and gain Soul-Heart boosts.

## Native evidence and limits

Final `work/lola-gen9-final.log`:4 groups/25 parameter cases, including a
complete-decision timing case. Actual compiled party generation is used.
Reserve-only cases promote the surviving last two members; they do not prove
the full party naturally reaches that exact pair. Native switches in the
lead cases remain unforced. NPC move choices are not scripted.

- Physical lead: Sash leaves Cherubi1HP after turn1 Sunny Day. Weather Ball
  and Energy Ball follow, alongside Leafeon's Leaf Blade. Both reference foes
  fall by turn3 with Cherubi1HP/Leafeon6HP and reserves untouched. Sunny Day
  PP remains4 throughout: one use, not repeated setup. With the original
  Heat Rock Cherubi died on turn1 and Ponyta had to replace it.
- Psychic/Electric lead: the native AI immediately switches Cherubi to Ponyta
  while Leafeon protects, then attacks and removes Natu on turn2. This is a
  weatherless fallback, not proof of a successful sun opening. Ponyta protects
  on turn3 while Leafeon attacks; the sample does not finish Pachirisu.
- Fake Out/Taunt attempt: Cherubi uses Helping Hand before Fake Out lands.
  Leafeon removes Mienfoo before its turn2 Taunt can execute; Cherubi then
  sets sun and attacks on turn3. Do not claim this proves recovery from a
  successfully applied Taunt.
- Real weather replacement: Prankster Sableye protects on turn1, allowing
  Cherubi sun/Leafeon Protect. Turn2 priority Rain Dance replaces sun before
  attacks; actual effective Grass speeds fall to34/56. On turn3 Cherubi
  restores sun and Leafeon removes Sableye, returning to68/112. Sunny Day PP
  changes4→4→3, with assertions on weather and Speed. That second use is
  useful restoration, not an accidental redundant recast. The earlier Lotad
  reference died before using Rain Dance and was rejected as denial evidence.
- Fast Fire lead pressure: Scarf Electrike's Flamethrower leaves Cherubi at
  Sash1HP; sun and Leaf Blade remove Electrike on turn1. Helping Hand plus
  Leafeon's attack finishes Eevee on turn2, though Leafeon falls too. No
  Flash Fire switch happened here; the old diagnostic title was corrected.
- Separate promoted Fire reserves: actual Flamethrower into Ponyta activates
  Flash Fire, asserted in native state. Ponyta remains full54HP on turn1 and
  removes Electrike with High Horsepower. Luvdisc Protect then Icy Wind and
  boosted Flare Blitz leave Eevee4HP on turn2. This verifies immunity and
  follow-through, not a predicted switch into an unseen player command.
- Promoted physical reserves: Draining Kiss plus Flare Blitz removes Eevee
  on turn2, but Ponyta falls; both faints give Luvdisc +2 SpA, asserted.
  Draining Kiss sustains Luvdisc to turn4 at5HP, with Timburr35HP. This is a
  remaining unfavorable reference, not a claimed win or general failure of
  the full four-member team.
- Promoted Psychic/Electric reserves: Protect/Flare Blitz, then Icy Wind/
  Flare Blitz removes Natu and gives Soul-Heart +1 SpA. Turn3 Heal Pulse
  heals Ponyta11→38HP before Luvdisc falls; its actual animation, PP10→9,
  and Ponyta's resulting HP are verified. Ponyta attacks with High Horsepower
  and finishes Pachirisu with Flare Blitz on turn4, retaining31HP.

## Rating

8.5/10, intention-based rather than a measured win rate. Better physical
reserve sustain and less conservative weatherless positioning would improve
Lola. The main sun engine, weather restoration, Flash Fire and partner healing
execute, while the reserves retain meaningful matchup weaknesses.
