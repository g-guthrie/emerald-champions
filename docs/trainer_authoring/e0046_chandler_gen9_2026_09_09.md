# E0046 Chandler — Gen 9 native review

Route109's event starts a standalone double with `TRAINER_CHANDLER`, not a
simultaneous encounter with the nearby trainers. Reviewed scope: first-access
Normal, one badge, cap20 and native level18 for all four opponents. This is
not later-return/difficulty-wide verification. Player references use native
level20, IV31, exact EVs and preparation-legal moves.

## Accepted changes and team logic

- Phione: Damp Rock → Sitrus Berry. Native62 maximum HP gives the convenient
  threshold32 and15HP recovery. It survives two ordinary36-damage hits rather
  than falling to the second hit after rain setup. Five-turn rather than
  eight-turn rain is the deliberate tradeoff; the observed lead finishes in
  three or four turns. Full special offense and Speed remain.
- Buizel: Life Orb → Eviolite. Its original physical offense/Speed spread
  remains. Extra durability and no Life Orb recoil retain its attacks in the
  reserve phase, without sacrificing the narrow unboosted Speed crossing.
- Joltik's Eviolite/Compound Eyes and Quagsire's Leftovers/Unaware are retained.
  All species, abilities, moves, level offsets, EVs and party order remain.
- No shared-AI changes. The existing executable RAIN plan establishes useful
  weather and rejects active-weather repetition. Updated plan prose records
  intentions and limitations; it is not itself an AI implementation.

## Rating

8.5/10, intention-based, not a measured win rate. Stronger closing pressure
against boosted sustain teams and better conditional Rest evaluation would
improve Chandler. The rain offense, Swift Swim, Hydration and Unaware motifs
execute without depending on perfect play or mandatory move sequences.
