- Mothim: Life Orb →Sitrus, Psychic →Protect. Retain Tinted Lens/Timid
  4HP252SpA252Spe, Quiver Dance, Bug Buzz and Air Slash. Give up immediate
  recoil-boosted damage and Psychic coverage for survival and useful protected
  turns while its partner attacks or establishes Body Press. Final native
  samples show actual single-Dance payoff, not repeated setup or guard spam.

Keep executable SETUP and the existing shared policy. No gameplay AI code was
changed for Derek. Coaching: apply immediate Ground/Grass/Bug pressure when
it wins the exchange; protect the threatened partner while the other acts.
Establish one useful defensive/offensive boost, then attack. Replace a fallen
lead with the appropriate reserve, preserving the all-Burmy-family identity.

The current canonical campaign has this single Sandy/Trash/Mothim trainer
appearance and one later Plant Wormadam reuse. It is a distinctive family team,
not generic weather filler. Preserve its substantial Fire/Flying counterplay;
