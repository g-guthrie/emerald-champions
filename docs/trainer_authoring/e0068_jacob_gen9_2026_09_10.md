## Demonstrated shared defect and correction

The extended burn/Follow Me replay exposed repeated failed Leech Seeds.
Ferroseed seeded Pachirisu on1, then targeted unseeded Sableye on2–4;
Follow Me redirected each attempt into already-seeded Pachirisu. The old
five-turn sample won, but its success did not excuse these wasted actions.

`src/battle_ai_main.c` now applies a soft20-point penalty when a living,
already-seeded partner of the intended target just revealed usable Follow Me
or Rage Powder. It excludes incapacitated/disabled redirectors, redirection
bypass and powder immunity through existing helpers. This is a prediction
from the last observed move, not access to pending player commands or a
guarantee of redirection. One four-slot scan; no new state or simulated turns.
It is deliberately narrow: not a general solution for every redirected move.

Fixed replay: Seed once, then Gyro Ball on2–4; Pachirisu falls on4. Cyclizar
finishes Sableye before the queued Seed on5 executes. Seed PP remains9.
The shared rule can affect other trainers; no campaign-wide retest is claimed.

One necessary frozen native regression, `EC Leech Seed: do not repeat into a
seeded Follow Me recipient`, checks the first Seed actually landed and that
the next turn spends Gyro Ball PP instead. Fixed log:
`work/jacob-seed-regression-fixed.log`. Reverting only this correction causes
the same test to fail with Seed PP8 instead of9 in
`work/jacob-seed-regression-counterfactual.log`. Correction restored afterward.
