Establish: Psychic Surge enables grounded Expanding Force; Indeedee chooses

not against grounded foes protected by the team's own field. Levitate means
Chimecho normally does not receive Psychic Terrain's offensive boost.

more deliberate Psychic Terrain restoration would improve Robin. The supported
spread opening and Competitive punishment execute well; the slow reserves
still have clear weaknesses and occasional overly conservative guards.
