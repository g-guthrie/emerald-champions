## Coaching card and composition

Establish: retain Life Orb Intimidate Jolly Krokorok4HP/252Atk/252Speed with
Crunch/Stomping Tantrum/Rock Slide/Protect. Intimidate supports Eviolite Overcoat
Bold Vullaby252HP/252Def/4SpD, whose Foul Play/Snarl/Roost/Taunt provide mixed
pressure reduction and longevity. Foul Play is not powered by Vullaby's own
Attack; Intimidate can also reduce that move's damage against the affected
opponent. Keep independent Ground/Dark pressure rather than treating the
support interaction as a free offensive boost. Vullaby's physical investment
remains justified by repeated Ice/contact pressure; special concentration is
still exploitable. Krokorok retains immediate power at a durability cost.

Exploit/fallback: retain Sitrus Blaze Timid Simisear4HP/252SpA/252Speed,
Heat Wave/Grass Knot/Will-O-Wisp/Protect. Replace Choice Specs Blaze Timid
Quilava4HP/252SpA/252Speed (Heat Wave/Flamethrower/Extrasensory/Overheat) with
Rindo Storm Drain Modest Gastrodon252HP/4Def/252SpA,
Earth Power/Ice Beam/Recover/Protect. Two Fire reserves shared Water/Rock
exposure; the baseline Fake Out/Scald reserve sample loses Quilava1 and
Simisear2. Gastrodon gives both Simisear and Krokorok single-target Water
protection, gains special offense from absorption, and has independent Ground/
Ice pressure. Simisear threatens Grass attackers; Rindo cushions one Grass hit.
Gastrodon does not pull every recipient of Surf out of harm and has no allied
Water activator on this team. This is defensive Storm Drain synergy, not an
allied activation claim or Water immunity for the entire party in every case.

The replacement costs fast Specs damage/Fire duplication and introduces a
fourfold Grass weakness and slow attacker. Modest/maxSpA makes the absorption
payoff meaningful; HP investment and Recover support repeated attacks, without
pretending to be maximum special bulk. Species variety improves relative to
nearby Jacob's Specs Quilava. Later Gastrodon sets mostly emphasize special
bulk, Icy Wind or Leftovers; this offensive Rindo reserve is not copied across
the campaign. All three retained sets keep their exact EVs/natures/items/moves.
