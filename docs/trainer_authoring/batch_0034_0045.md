# Trainer authoring: E0034–E0045

September 8, 2026. Twelve branches, 62 party slots; E0036 contains both
Brenden and Lilith, and E0041 is intentionally absent. Edited only these
canonical team blocks. No materialization, core AI edit, build, or battle run
was performed by this author in this pass. The build owner must materialize
the changes before any ROM can contain them.

Ratings below measure intended encounter design, not win rates or test coverage.
Every branch was individually reviewed. Runtime uncertainty is recorded separately
from the concrete design limitations behind ratings below 10.

## Executable evidence and shared constraints

- `src/trainer_util.c::GenerateMonFromTrainerMon` consumes authored nature,
  six stat investments, four moves, item, and explicit species ability. Ability
  legality was checked against the **preprocessed configured species table**, not
  a historical team sheet or online species description.
- `src/pokemon.c::CalculateMonStatsCont` and `include/config/pokemon.h` use
  Champions stats: perfect IV 31, then fixed Stat Points before nature. These
  are not ordinary EVs divided by four. Every allocation here totals 66, with
  at most 32 in a stat. All 248 moves were checked against the actual compiled
  preparation corpus in `src/data/pokemon/emerald_champions_preparation_learnsets.h`.
- `src/caps.c` gives cap 20 after Roxanne and before Brawly, then 30 after Brawly.
  `src/difficulty.c::ApplyTrainerLevelDifficulty` floors authored levels at the
  **live** cap, then subtracts 4/2/0 on Easy/Normal/Hard. Negative authored
  offsets do not remain a below-cap hierarchy. All authored offsets and party
  counts are preserved. The numerical opening benchmarks below assume live cap
  20; returns at a higher cap require recomputation.
- `src/battle_ai_pair.c` evaluates legal allied targets, weather/Tailwind order,
  protection, damage and supported partnerships. `plan`/`crack` are explanations,
  not executable scripts. Coaching is legal `TARGET_ALLY`/`EFFECT_STAT_CHANGE`,
  with conditional ally-stat scoring in `battle_ai_main.c` and
  `battle_ai_util.c::GetAllyStatChangeScore`; the pair evaluator does not fully
  simulate every Coaching or multi-turn trapping payoff.
- The only explicit ACTIVATE instructions in this batch remain Cristian's two
  Beat Up recipients. No unsupported tactic names or forced enemy choices were
  invented. Normal reserve choices remain conditional, except existing ace locks.

Useful counterplay is physically available before the gym, without assuming
Surf, Good Rod, Super Rod, a Mega Ring, or a particular evolved form:
`gRoute101_LandMons` has Mienfoo and Taillow; `gRoute102_LandMons` has Ralts,
Natu, Lotad, Togepi and Pachirisu. The current preparation table provides
Mienfoo Fake Out/Taunt/Feint, Ralts Psychic/Taunt/Trick Room, Natu Psychic/
Air Slash/Tailwind/Haze, Taillow Brave Bird/Tailwind, Lotad Energy Ball/Rain Dance,
Togepi Dazzling Gleam/Follow Me, and Pachirisu Nuzzle/Follow Me. These are options,
not guaranteed winning teams. Old Rod Mantyke offers Wide Guard and Tailwind;
its first-two-slot Dewford fishing entry is not a claim that later rods are
already available. Granite Cave's native Emerald tables also provide Abra and
Sableye; the opening-route options above avoid depending on dark-floor traversal.

Spread shorthand is expanded by `scripts/emerald_champions_teams.py`:
PS/SS maximize offensive stat and Speed; PB/SB maximize HP and offensive stat;
WD/WS emphasize HP and one defense; WM is 32/0/16/0/18/0; FS is
32/0/2/0/0/32. Order is HP/Atk/Def/SpA/SpD/Spe.

## E0034 — TRAINER_ELLIOT_1 — 8/10

**Authored change:** Frillish's Hex becomes Icy Wind. Clauncher/Frillish remain
the lead. Clauncher's Modest SS, Specs and Mega Launcher reward immediate pulse
damage; Frillish's Bold WD/Eviolite supports repeated speed control, burns and
recovery instead of competing for a second conditional attack. Its low Speed
means the first Icy Wind is primarily a subsequent-action/turn investment.
Water Absorb is useful immunity, not an invented ally-heal instruction.

Shellder retains White Herb/Skill Link/Jolly PS and Shell Smash, with two
multi-hit coverage moves. It should set up only when the field permits; White
Herb restores defense drops but is not damage immunity. Corphish retains Band,
Adaptability, Adamant fast investment and Aqua Jet as immediate closing pressure
when setup is unsafe. Knock Off and X-Scissor provide alternatives to Water lock.

Counterplay: Lotad's Grass pressure, Pachirisu's Electric pressure, or Mienfoo's
Taunt into support/setup; account for Ice coverage rather than assuming Grass
wins outright. Wide Guard can deny Icy Wind but not the single-target pulses.
The Water identity deliberately repeats a typing, not another trainer's full plan.

**Why not 10 / improvement path:** Frillish is still the sole enabler for two
fragile offensive reserve choices. A more independent bulky closing mode could
give a meaningful second plan when speed control is denied; the present roster
instead chooses an aggressive fisherman identity. Runtime debt: no fresh
encounter test of setup timing or Frillish survival.

## E0035 — TRAINER_LAURA — 8/10

**Authored changes:** Mienfoo now opens alongside Timburr, not Croagunk. Fake Out
can buy Flame Orb activation; Timburr can also Protect while Mienfoo attacks or
pivots. Eviolite Regenerator Mienfoo keeps Jolly PS, Drain Punch, Knock Off and
U-turn: useful fast pressure plus an actual retreat tool. Guts Timburr keeps
Adamant PB, Facade/Drain Punch/Mach Punch/Protect, trading speed for activated
bulk, healing and priority.

Croagunk retains Black Sludge/Dry Skin and Poison Jab as a Fairy-facing reserve,
with Fake Out for its own entry, not repeat use without switching. Makuhita's
Brick Break becomes Wide Guard; its remaining Knock Off keeps PB Attack useful
while Fake Out/Protect offer distinct defensive choices. Scarf Jolly Mankey
retains Defiant and four attacking/pivot moves as the fast response to careless
Intimidate. Tyrogue changes from unsupported Leftovers Guts attacker to
Sash/Vital Spirit/Jolly FS with Fake Out, Feint, Helping Hand and Counter.
HP/Speed support utility and physical Counter opportunities; Vital Spirit
directly answers sleep rather than waiting for an unplanned status activation.

Counterplay: Ralts/Natu special Psychic avoids Counter and physical bulk;
Protect or a Ghost switch can deny Fake Out, but an ordinary switch cannot.
Taillow offers Flying pressure, while opposing spread attacks must account for
Makuhita. The six unevolved/baby fighters are retained, not replaced by six
fully evolved stat upgrades.

**Why not 10 / improvement path:** several reserves still compete for the same
entry-pressure role. A dedicated alternative speed-control mode would make the
middle phase more distinct, at the cost of one Fake Out specialist. Runtime
debt: activation, pivot and Wide Guard choices have not been played individually.

## E0036 — TRAINER_BRENDEN — 8/10

**Authored changes:** Prankster Riolu moves into the opening next to Scarf Sawk
and changes PS to FS. Coaching can precede Sawk without spending Riolu's points
on weak damage; Sash, Protect and priority Bullet Punch preserve useful fallback
actions. Sawk retains Adamant PS, Mold Breaker and immediate physical coverage;
its Choice lock is a deliberate commitment, and Mold Breaker bypasses applicable
abilities, not type immunities.

Throh's Circle Throw becomes Storm Throw: a normal-priority, accurate critical
attack to contest Defense boosts instead of phazing away a target Sawk pressured.
Leftovers/Guts/PB and Bulk Up remain a bulky reserve mode; Guts deters status but
is not self-activated. No Guard Machop retains Eviolite, Dynamic Punch, Knock Off
and priority. Stufful retains Fluffy/Sitrus/Bulk Up as a contact-facing alternative;
Monferno's AV, Jolly PS, Fire Punch and U-turn supply special bulk, Fire coverage
and a pivot rather than another setup requirement.

Counterplay: special Psychic/Fairy pressure ignores Coaching's Defense increase;
Fake Out/Taunt can interrupt Riolu, and multiple hits or double targeting beat
Sash. Non-contact Fire is particularly useful against Fluffy. All six physical
attackers/supporters fit Coaching, unlike an arbitrary mixed recipient pool.

**Why not 10 / improvement path:** the entire damage plan is still physical,
and Riolu's removal leaves largely independent bruisers. A future special
Monferno variant could provide a second axis but would trade away Coaching's
offensive benefit and Fire Punch/physical pivot consistency. Runtime debt:
Coaching target/order and setup choices have not been exercised in this encounter.

## E0036 — TRAINER_LILITH — 8/10

Hitmontop/Scraggy remain the opening: Helmet/Intimidate/Fake Out can make room
for Eviolite Moxie Scraggy's Dragon Dance. Hitmontop retains PB Attack for Close
Combat alongside Feint and Helping Hand; Scraggy retains fast Adamant investment
so one boost matters. **Changes:** Pancham gains Parting Shot over Body Slam,
creating an actual pivot back into Hitmontop if it has left. Life Orb/Scrappy
and Fighting/Dark damage retain pressure into Ghost-oriented answers.

Leek Galarian Farfetch'd replaces redundant Brick Break with Leaf Blade.
The configured critical-stage system combines Leek's +2 with a high-crit move's
+1, subject to crit-blocking effects. Meditite retains Sitrus/Pure Power, Fake
Out and dual STAB as immediate reserve damage. Clobbopus gains Eviolite and
Circle Throw instead of Leftovers/Brick Break: native 60 power receives
Technician, but 90 accuracy and -6 priority are explicit costs. Feint/Taunt
provide different answers when phazing is wrong or unavailable.

Counterplay: special attacks bypass Intimidate; Ralts/Togepi Fairy pressure
targets Scraggy; Defiant/Competitive can punish both Intimidate and Parting Shot
when available. Protect is not a universal answer to Feint. This is the gym's
pivot/setup branch, not Brenden's direct Coaching branch.

**Why not 10 / improvement path:** only Pancham supplies an explicit pivot, so
cycling requires it and Hitmontop to remain useful. A second pivot or a more
independent special reserve would deepen the recovery phase, but would dilute
one of the present six Fighting specialties. Runtime debt: no claim that the
AI has executed a particular multi-turn Intimidate cycle.

## E0037 — TRAINER_TAKAO — 8/10

Grapploct/Wobbuffet retain a pressure-and-reaction opening. **Changes:** Limber
replaces Grapploct's inactive Technician: Drain Punch and Ice Punch are 75 power,
so the old ability boosted neither. PB/Leftovers sustain Octolock plus attacks.
Wobbuffet retains WD/Sitrus, Counter/Mirror Coat, Encore and Safeguard; it needs
an opponent's actionable choice rather than magically choosing damage freely.
`BS_TrySetOctolock` sets the volatile/defense-drain independently of the general
Ghost escape rule; the former prose that it simply misses Ghosts was false.

Hitmonlee keeps Jolly PS/White Herb and Close Combat to trigger Unburden, with
Fake Out on entry and Dark/Fire coverage. Poliwrath replaces redundant Close
Combat with Liquidation, keeping Drain Punch, AV, Water Absorb and bulky Attack.
Passimian retains Scarf/Defiant/U-turn as an independent fast response. Pawmo
gains Eviolite and Nuzzle over Life Orb/Knock Off; Jolly PS still powers Iron
Fist attacks while paralysis helps the slower trap core.

Counterplay: Taunt from an early Ralts or Mienfoo, Ghost escape such as Sableye,
or a legal pivot can contest trapping. Psychic special offense pressures the
fighters, but predictable attacks feed Wobbuffet's retaliation. Octolock is not
a guaranteed lock-and-KO script and Wide Guard does not stop single-target damage.

**Why not 10 / improvement path:** Wobbuffet's reactive opening gives a patient
support/setup opponent room to maneuver. An active direct-damage partner phase
could shorten that window, but replacing the Wobbuffet opening would change the
trainer's distinctive trap/reaction lesson. Runtime debt: trap persistence,
Encore selections and reserve transitions need encounter-level tests.

## E0038 — TRAINER_CRISTIAN — 8.5/10

**Changes:** Falinks becomes Jolly FS, replacing No Retreat with Protect.
Gallade replaces Bulk Up with Protect: safe positioning is more useful beside
potential multi-hit Justified than another Attack boost. Lucario becomes
5/32/0/0/0/29 instead of PS, trading three Speed points for HP and reliable
initial activation order. Falinks/Gallade remain the lead; Lucario is the second
explicit ACTIVATE recipient. Protect is not a command to hit a protected ally.

The actual code distinction matters: `B_BEAT_UP = GEN_LATEST` selects
`CalcBeatUpPower`, using each participant's **species base Attack** /10 +5 for
power. Modern Beat Up then uses `CalcAttackStat` and `CalculateBaseDamage` with
**Falinks's actual Attack**. Lower Attack investment therefore reduces ally
damage, not per-hit base power. The base-stat-only damage routine is pre-Gen5.

Initial-cap unmodified Speed, Easy/Normal/Hard:

| Pokémon | Easy | Normal | Hard |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Falinks | 71 | 75 | 80 |
| Gallade | 69 | 73 | 77 |
| Lucario | 69 | 74 | 78 |

These are source-formula calculations, not battle observations. Status, items,
stages, priority, Trick Room and a later live cap still override this comparison.
The actual AI survival/order checks remain authoritative. Lucario's priority
attacks can intentionally occur before activation; they are not automatically
the correct moves on an activation turn.

Gallade retains Life Orb and physical dual STAB/healing; Lucario retains Expert
Belt, physical coverage and two priority choices. Hitmonchan retains AV/PB/Iron
Fist coverage. Existing Double Ace locks keep Band/No Guard Machamp and bulky
Leftovers/Vital Spirit Annihilape last; their reliability and Rage Fist/Bulk Up
mode make sense without Falinks surviving.

Counterplay: Fake Out/Taunt on Falinks, Follow Me redirecting an eligible attack,
or Haze/speed control on the boosted phase; do not assume killing Gallade ends
the plan. **Why not 10 / improvement path:** both activation recipients and
both aces still attack the same physical axis. A special Lucario alternative
would diversify damage but would no longer be a meaningful Justified recipient;
that is a real partnership tradeoff, not a missing test. Runtime debt: no new
damage roll or live activation result is claimed for these investments.

## E0039 — TRAINER_JOCELYN — 8.5/10

Whimsicott/Combusken remain the opening. Light Clay/Prankster/Tailwind/Light
Screen give Whimsicott a special-defense and speed role, with Encore/Moonblast
for other boards. **Changes:** Combusken trades eight Speed points for eight HP
(10/32/0/0/0/24), retaining Jolly, Life Orb, Speed Boost, Swords Dance and Protect.
At cap 20 its Easy/Normal/Hard Speed is 57/60/63, or 114/120/126 in Tailwind.
That exceeds a cap-20 max-Speed positive-nature base-151 Pheromosa's 113 before
other modifiers. This is a specific existing fast-threat benchmark, not a claim
that Combusken outruns every possible boosted/scarfed player.

Volbeat retains Mental Herb/Prankster/FS as replacement speed support, with
Thunder Wave, Helping Hand and Bug Buzz. Toxic Orb/Poison Heal Breloom keeps
Spore, Protect and dual STAB: a status-resilient reserve distinct from Combusken.
Band/Iron Fist/PB Crabominable spends the speed window on immediate attacks;
Ice Hammer's Speed drop is a real cost. The locked Sirfetch'd ace replaces
recharge-inducing Meteor Assault with Leek Leaf Blade while keeping Scrappy,
Close Combat, Knock Off and entry-only First Impression.

Counterplay: early Natu/Taillow Tailwind, Ralts Trick Room on a suitable board,
or pressure into the support slots. Dark immunity blocks targeted opposing
Prankster moves, not Tailwind/Light Screen. **Why not 10 / improvement path:**
the two support reserves largely renew the same speed plan, rather than offer
a different endgame when speed control is contested. A non-speed defensive
Volbeat mode could diversify it, sacrificing the deliberate Tailwind backup.
Runtime debt: no updated turn-order or reserve-selection battle run.

## E0040 — TRAINER_BRAWLY_1 — 9/10

Pachirisu/Heracross retain the leader's redirection/offense opening. **Changes:**
Pachirisu shifts to 32/0/18/0/16/0 Impish instead of heavily special-biased WS.
Its initial Easy/Normal/Hard Defense/SpD become 58/58, 62/63, 66/67; the same HP,
Sitrus, Volt Absorb, Follow Me/Nuzzle/Super Fang/Helping Hand remain. This makes
physical redirection credible without discarding special bulk. Heracross retains
Band/Moxie/Adamant PS, immediate Fighting/Bug damage and coverage: a strong
opening threat with deliberate lock and accuracy costs.

Conkeldurr and Hariyama become Adamant rather than Brave: no Trick Room plan
justifies lowering Speed when Nuzzle is present. Conkeldurr retains Flame Orb/
Guts/Facade, Drain Punch, Mach Punch and Knock Off. Hariyama retains AV/Thick Fat,
Fake Out, Close Combat, Bullet Punch and Knock Off as an immediate bulk/entry
mode. Hawlucha keeps White Herb/Unburden and Jolly PS: Close Combat consumes the
item, enabling both Speed and itemless Acrobatics, with Swords Dance/Protect
as conditional alternatives.

The final locked Cobalion ace changes to **Timid SS, Aura Sphere/Flash Cannon/
Quick Guard/Coaching**. Its configured base Attack and SpA are both 90. Special
STAB now contests physical walls and burns without lowering its prior Speed;
Quick Guard and Coaching still assist a surviving physical partner. Leftovers
supports repeated actions. Justified is its only configured ability, but an
Attack increase is no longer advertised as offensive payoff for this set.

Counterplay: Dazzling Gleam or other spread damage bypasses Follow Me; Mienfoo's
Feint can contest protection, while Natu's special offense pressures the core.
Burn/Intimidate are not universal answers to Guts or special Cobalion. Ordinary
Ground/Fire moves bypass Quick Guard. **Why not 10 / improvement path:** the
middle bulky-fighter phase has less sharply differentiated support than the
opening and special ace. A dedicated spread-protection Hariyama mode could
deepen that phase, but would require dropping AV and one current priority/coverage
role. The current choice deliberately retains special bulk. Runtime debt:
no full fresh leader battle or quantified survival claim for these new spreads.

## E0042 — TRAINER_HAILEY — 8/10

**Change:** Flaaffy shifts Bold WS to Bold WM. At live cap 20 its
Easy/Normal/Hard defenses are balanced at 47/47, 50/50, 53/53 before screens.
This avoids a needless physical hole on the dual-screen setter. Light Clay,
Static, Electroweb and Thunderbolt remain: immediate speed/damage choices
exist when spending two turns on screens is wrong.

Marill keeps Sitrus/Huge Power/PB, Play Rough, Aqua Jet, Brick Break and Protect;
it is the immediate physical partner, not a setup passenger. Tympole retains
Eviolite/Water Absorb/SB and Water/Ground/Poison special coverage. Wartortle keeps
White Herb/Modest SS, Shell Smash, Hydro Pump, Ice Beam and Protect; it is the
reserve investment, not an obligatory turn-one choice. This screen-backed
shell setup is distinct from Elliot's burn/speed-control offensive fisherman.

Counterplay: Taunt Flaaffy, use Lotad's Grass pressure on Water reserves, or
remove screens with a legal Brick Break user such as Mienfoo. Electric immunity
can contest Electroweb but does not remove the screens already established.
**Why not 10 / improvement path:** Hailey has one pivotal support Pokémon and
no pivot, making screened board preservation less flexible. A reserve support
move instead of one coverage attack could strengthen recovery after Flaaffy
falls, at the cost of the current broad offensive coverage. Runtime debt:
individual screen/setup timing and Shell Smash survival remain unmeasured.

## E0043 — TRAINER_EDMOND — 8/10

**Changes:** Mantyke/Krabby now lead; Mantyke gets Eviolite and Tailwind over
Sitrus/Air Slash. Calm WS emphasizes its already strong special bulk; Scald,
Helping Hand and Protect remain. Krabby replaces Crabhammer with Liquidation:
native 85-power Liquidation has a secondary Defense-drop chance, so Sheer Force
boosts it and removes that effect. Rock Slide also qualifies; X-Scissor does
not. Life Orb/Adamant PS maximize the payoff but do not grant every move the
Sheer Force recoil exception.

Koffing becomes Levitate instead of Neutralizing Gas. The old suppression had
no authored positive partner and switched off all three partners' useful
abilities. Bold WD/Eviolite, burn, Clear Smog and Protect now form a coherent
physical/setup-checking reserve with Ground immunity. Staravia retains Scarf/
Reckless/Adamant PS, Brave Bird, Double-Edge, U-turn and Quick Attack; it can be
fast after Tailwind ends, with Choice and recoil as costs rather than a claim
that it necessarily defeats itself.

Counterplay: Pachirisu/Electric pressure threatens the Water lead; Taunt stops
Mantyke support, and burns contest Krabby. Psychic attacks pressure Koffing,
but ordinary Ground attacks do not bypass Levitate. **Why not 10 / improvement
path:** Mantyke is slow and cannot guarantee establishing Tailwind before attacks.
The team must buy that turn with Protect or a favorable matchup. A faster
support spread could contest selected opposing support speeds, sacrificing the
chosen special bulk; that trade needs a named matchup, not arbitrary max Speed.
Runtime debt: no new Tailwind/Helping Hand encounter-level selection test.

## E0044 — TRAINER_RICKY_1 — 8/10

**Changes:** Chewtle's Crunch becomes Jaw Lock, preserving 80-power Dark biting
damage and Strong Jaw while offering mutual trapping beside Wooper's Yawn.
This requires a successful hit and a useful target, and also traps Chewtle.
Wooper retains Impish WD/Eviolite, Water Absorb, recovery and dual STAB; Chewtle
keeps Life Orb/Adamant PS, Water STAB, Ice Fang and Protect. Neither is promised
to survive every attack while a slow sleep plan develops.

Prinplup retains Sash/Competitive/Modest SS, Hydro Pump, Ice Beam, Icy Wind and
Protect: special pressure and speed control if the lead collapses. The repeated
Specs Clauncher becomes Scope Lens/Sniper/Modest SS Remoraid with Focus Energy,
Water Pulse, Ice Beam and Protect. Modern crit-stage code gives Focus Energy +2
and Scope Lens +1; stage 3 guarantees a critical subject to prevention. Native
modern critical damage and Sniper are each 1.5 multipliers, not a made-up damage
rule. `AI_CheckViability` explicitly rewards Focus Energy with Sniper/Scope Lens.

Counterplay: Lotad Grass pressure, Taunt or sleep immunity, protecting the target
of Jaw Lock, and priority/double-target pressure on the frail critical attacker.
Early Natu Haze can remove ordinary stat boosts but **does not** remove Focus
Energy's volatile critical boost. **Why not 10 / improvement path:** Jaw Lock/
Yawn is a soft opportunity, not a bespoke synchronized trapping instruction.
A dedicated executable same-target preference could make this unique opening
more intentional, provided it still rejects lethal/immune/protected boards.
Runtime debt: no native demonstration of the two-move sleep/trap sequence.

## E0045 — TRAINER_LOLA_1 — 8.5/10

Cherubi/Leafeon keep the manual-sun opening. **Changes:** Cherubi replaces
Dazzling Gleam with Weather Ball, giving its own Modest SS/Chlorophyll/Heat Rock
set an offensive sunlight payoff alongside Energy Ball and Helping Hand.
Leafeon replaces Quick Attack with Protect, retaining Life Orb/Adamant PS and
three coverage attacks; protecting during sun setup is an option, not mandatory.

The reserve phase was rebuilt rather than merely documenting its old conflict.
Soul-Heart Luvdisc replaces sun-weakened Hydro Pump with Heal Pulse; Timid SS,
Sash, Icy Wind, Draining Kiss and Protect let it support an attacker while
converting faints into meaningful special pressure. Ferroseed is replaced by
**Eviolite Flash Fire Ponyta**, Jolly 10/24/0/0/0/32, with Flare Blitz, High
Horsepower, Morning Sun and Protect. It provides a Fire-immune reserve instead
of a sun-amplified Fire weakness. Eight Attack points move to HP versus PS to
support recoil/recovery while retaining full Speed; sun supplies the offensive
Fire multiplier. Native Morning Sun restores two-thirds max HP under ordinary
sun, half without weather and a quarter under other applicable weather. Flash
Fire does not force the AI to predict an unseen incoming Fire attack.

Counterplay: interrupt Cherubi, replace sun with Lotad's Rain Dance, pressure
the support slot, or use Ground/Rock attacks into Ponyta. Fire alone is not a
universal answer to this Grass opening. Luvdisc's Heal Pulse does not heal itself.
**Why not 10 / improvement path:** the reserve pair is now coherent, but the
manual setter remains the only weather source. A second independent weather
option would improve recovery after early denial, at the cost of support or
coverage; the current version intentionally leaves this exploitable seam.
Runtime debt: no new weather-reserve or Heal Pulse sequence has been played.

## Distribution and verification record

The final batch has **62 species across 62 slots**. The two species replacements
are Ricky's duplicate Clauncher → Remoraid and Lola's conflicting Ferroseed →
Ponyta. This is not a uniqueness quota: all seven Fighting-gym branches retain
their identities and differentiated unevolved, evolved, support and ace roles.
Elliot, Hailey and Ricky deliberately remain coastal Water-heavy encounters,
but use speed/burn, screens/setup and sleep-trap/critical pressure respectively.
Edmond uses marine support plus bird/poison reserves; Lola supplies a different
weather axis without pretending Water typing itself benefits from sun.

immutable targets. Jocelyn's Breloom was retained for the explicitly Poison Heal

Clauncher twice, Remoraid once, Ferroseed once and Ponyta twice campaign-wide.
