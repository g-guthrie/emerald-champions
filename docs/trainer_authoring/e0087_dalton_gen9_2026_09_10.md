  Draco Meteor/Tailwind/Super Fang/Taunt. Tailwind is conditional support,
  followed by damage; surviving a hit at1HP can buy the establishment turn.
- Exploit: retain Eviolite Soundproof Modest Loudred252HP/4Def/252SpA,
  Hyper Voice/Flamethrower/Icy Wind/Protect. Spread damage and coverage are its
  foundation. Soundproof specifically blocks opposing sound moves such as
  Snarl; this is not an invented allied Boomburst partnership.
- Physical support reserve: Stantler keeps Intimidate, Jolly4HP/252Atk/252Speed,
  Double-Edge/Psyshield Bash/Protect. Clear Amulet becomes Sitrus, trading
  stat-drop immunity for recovery after pressure/recoil. Thunder Wave becomes
  native-teachable Helping Hand: a finishing partner attack can matter more
  than another layer of Speed control. Psyshield Bash supplies physical bulk
  while attacking; no compulsory setup turn is added.
- Cleaner: Yanma retains Speed Boost, Modest4HP/252SpA/252Speed and
  Bug Buzz/Air Slash/Protect/Giga Drain. Life Orb becomes Eviolite, trading the
  damage multiplier for bulk and eliminating recoil. This distinguishes it
  from nearby Melina's Life Orb Speed Boost/Costar donor rather than replacing
  every Yanma. Later Clear Amulet Stantler and differently authored Loudred/
  Noibat sets remain intact. Guitarist identity and all four species stay.
