## Team and coaching card

- Establish: retain Eviolite/Battery/Sassy Charjabug252HP/252Def/4SpD,
  Discharge, Bug Bite, Eerie Impulse, Protect. Native Battery increases its
  partner's special damage; it does not boost Charjabug itself. Defensive
  investment preserves that passive support. Slow Discharge can restore an
  injured Volt Absorb partner after faster attacks; Bug Bite supplies physical
  coverage/berry removal. Eerie Impulse remains optional special disruption,
  not newly demonstrated execution in these samples.
- Exploit: Leftovers/Volt Absorb/Modest Lanturn keeps Thunderbolt, Hydro Pump,
  Ice Beam and Protect. Change252HP/4Def/252SpA to164HP/92Def/252SpA:
  HP134→128 and Defense46→52, retaining SpA86, SpD61 and Speed51. Approximate
  HP×Defense increases8%; HP×SpD decreases4.5%. Leftovers remains8HP per turn;
  Volt Absorb's quarter-HP heal changes33→32. This is explicit physical/special
  bulk allocation, not automatic berry parity adjustment or a damage upgrade.
- First reserves: retain Sitrus/Static/Modest Stunfisk252HP/4Def/252SpA,
  Discharge, Earth Power, Yawn, Protect; retain Specs/Volt Absorb/Timid
  Jolteon4HP/252SpA/252Speed, Thunderbolt, Shadow Ball, Volt Switch, Hyper Voice.
  Ground coverage and fast special coverage cooperate; Stunfisk's Discharge
  can heal Jolteon. Choice lock and their shared Ground weakness remain costs.
- Last reserves: Life Orb becomes Focus Sash on Dry Skin/Timid
  Heliolisk4HP/252SpA/252Speed; Thunderbolt, Grass Knot, Hyper Voice, Protect
  remain. Less damage and no recoil, but a full-health Ground/Fighting hit
  need not deny its follow-up. Chip/multihit defeats the Sash. Dry Skin remains
  Water immunity/healing with extra Fire vulnerability; no invented rain team.
  Keep Assault Vest/Levitate/Modest Vikavolt252HP/4Def/252SpA with Thunderbolt,
  Bug Buzz, Energy Ball and Volt Switch. It supplies special bulk, Ground
  immunity and strong independent attacks after Battery is lost.
