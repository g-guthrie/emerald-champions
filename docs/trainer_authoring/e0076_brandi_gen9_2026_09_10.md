## Changes and coaching

- Musharna: Calm Mind→Dazzling Gleam. Retain Leftovers/Synchronize/Quiet
  252HP4Def252SpA, Psychic, Yawn and Protect. The old reserve could not damage
  Dark types at all. Preserve Yawn as disruption beside a trapping partner;
  Kirlia remains the team's optional Calm Mind attacker.
- Wobbuffet: Destiny Bond→Helping Hand. Retain Sitrus/Shadow Tag/Calm
  252HP4Def252SpD, Counter, Mirror Coat and Encore. It can now improve its
  partner's actual attack without requiring the opponent to hit Wobbuffet.
  This trades conditional Destiny Bond for reliable supporting capability;
  later Wobbuffet sets still retain the trade mechanic.
- Bronzor retains Eviolite/Levitate/Relaxed252HP252Def4SpD, Gyro Ball,
  Helping Hand, Trick Room and Reflect. Kirlia retains Eviolite/Trace/Quiet
  252HP4Def252SpA, Psychic, Dazzling Gleam, Calm Mind and Protect.

Keep existing TRICK_ROOM/SETUP intentions and shared evaluator. No new AI
branches, search or runtime tables. Establish Room only when useful; then
attack or provide worthwhile support instead of reversing it. Recover from
interruption with coverage or a later setup window. Preserve slow reserve
entry, and let Wobbuffet choose direct support rather than assuming every
Counter or Mirror Coat will receive the required attack.

Current canonical distribution has this sole Bronzor, two other Kirlia,
several Musharna and Wobbuffet appearances. Here Musharna's special investment
and Yawn distinguish it from the specially defensive Calm Mind/Fairy version
in Jaclyn's team and later support versions. All four species, stat spreads,
items, abilities, level rules, IV31 and berry convenience are unchanged.
