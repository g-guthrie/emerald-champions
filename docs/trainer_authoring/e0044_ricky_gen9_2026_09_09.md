# E0044 Ricky — Gen 9 native review

Route109 starts a standalone double battle with `TRAINER_RICKY_1` and then
registers Match Call; its rematch event also uses this ID. This review covers
the first-access Normal, one-badge battle (cap20, all four opponents natively
level18), not a later rematch/return run. Player references are native level20,
IV31 and legal preparation sets.

## Accepted authoring

- Chewtle: Life Orb → Eviolite. Its Strong Jaw, original EVs and four moves
  remain. Better durability avoids the demonstrated immediate loss under
  concentrated Electric pressure and improves sustained physical attacks.
- Prinplup: Focus Sash → Eviolite. Keep Competitive and its original offensive/
  Speed EVs. The bulk helps it survive ordinary repeated hits and execute
  Icy Wind or exploit an opposing stat drop, rather than depending on full HP.
- Remoraid → Octillery, retaining Scope Lens/Sniper/Modest, the original
  4HP/252SpA/252Spe spread, Focus Energy/Water Pulse/Ice Beam/Protect, and

- Wooper, its Eviolite/Water Absorb/Impish physical bulk, Liquidation,
  Stomping Tantrum, Yawn and Recover remain unchanged.

  Actual native replacements are not forced to follow fixed pairs.
  No accepted EV, ability, move, authored-level, stat-rule or shared-AI change.

## Why the shared setup experiment was withdrawn

The authored setup preference only adds its bonus to stat-raising moves, not
Focus Energy. A small+35 Focus Energy preference was tested, then rejected
after comparing outcomes rather than treating setup itself as success.

With the bonus, the promoted physical reserve used Focus Energy on turn2,
lost Prinplup, removed Eevee, and then lost Octillery on turn4 with Timburr
still47HP. Without the bonus, direct attacks finished both foes in four turns
while retaining Prinplup28HP/Octillery33HP. The desirable Competitive/Focus
Energy scenario already executes without this new reward.

The temporary expectation demanding turn2 Focus Energy therefore tested the
wrong tactical requirement. It was removed, and the extra shared-code branch
was completely withdrawn. The retained code uses the original shared policy.
A source classification gap alone was not proof that another reward improved
this team. No permanent regression was added for the withdrawn experiment.

Other rejected candidates: leading Octillery beside Wooper, Surf replacing
Water Pulse, and Helping Hand replacing Wooper's Liquidation. The native AI
often immediately substituted Prinplup; these changes did not improve the
observed core. They are all withdrawn. No Surf/Water Absorb lead is claimed.

## Final native evidence

`work/ricky-gen9-final-baseline-policy.log`:4 groups/30 parameter cases.
The Competitive/Focus scenario asserts both the boost state and an actual
Water Pulse animation followed by the critical-hit message. This is not a
forced critical-hit roll or a roster-only inference.

- Ordinary physical lead: Chewtle protects first, then direct attacks remove
  Eevee on turn2. Wooper recovers while Chewtle supplies a useful allied
  Liquidation into Water Absorb; the third turn restores Wooper to64HP.
  Chewtle falls on turn4 and Prinplup enters. This sample does not finish
  Timburr and is not a claimed win.
- Psychic/Electric lead: the AI preserves Chewtle by actually switching to
  Prinplup on turn2. The final Eviolite Prinplup retains34HP rather than the
  original Sash version's23. Natu falls on turn3. Wooper uses Yawn on turn4;
  Pachirisu is asleep on turn5. No coordinated Jaw Lock trap was established.
- Fake Out/Taunt/Electric: the final team preserves Chewtle by switching, with
  no trainer faint through turn3; the original Life Orb Chewtle was lost on
  turn2. Taunt leaves Wooper using attacks. This is bounded disruption evidence,
  not proof that the team always wins the matchup.
- Follow Me counterplay diverts targeting; Prinplup still attacks and leaves
  the redirector2HP on turn3 in the final ordinary-move version. The player
  can meaningfully obstruct the lead's preferred targets.
- Slow Psychic reference: the team removes Eevee, then Wooper recovers while
  Chewtle uses allied Liquidation to heal Water Absorb Wooper. The target log
  explicitly identifies the ally; these are not repeated failed Water moves.
  The two healing actions are conservative and are not evidence of trapping.
- Promoted physical reserves: with the unmodified policy, direct Hydro Pump/
  Water Pulse offense finishes the pair on turn4; Prinplup28HP/Octillery33HP.
  It does not waste a Focus Energy turn just to match the written motif.
- Promoted Psychic/Electric reserves: Ice Beam pressure, Icy Wind, then attacks
  finish both foes on turn4, Prinplup4HP/Octillery27HP. Original Remoraid/Sash
  Prinplup lost both members by turn2 without a player knockout.
- Opposing Icy Wind: Competitive raises Prinplup's SpA by two stages while
  Octillery establishes Focus Energy on turn1. Prinplup's boosted Ice Beam
  removes Lotad on turn2; Octillery finishes Pachirisu with an actual critical
  Water Pulse on turn3. Both remain alive, Prinplup2HP/Octillery56HP.
  Focus Energy PP stays19 across the sequence, with no repeat setup.
  Under the compiled Gen7+ critical odds, Focus Energy's two stages plus Scope
  Lens's one give guaranteed critical hits unless a native blocker applies.
  The native critical-hit message, not that formula alone, proves execution.

## Rating

8/10, intention-based, not a measured win rate. More deliberate Yawn/Jaw Lock
coordination and less conservative opening positioning would improve Ricky.
The improved reserves deliver the critical-hit and Competitive motifs, while
the lead still has room for more purposeful pressure and trap coordination.
