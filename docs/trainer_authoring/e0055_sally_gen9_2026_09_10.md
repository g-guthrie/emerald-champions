# E0055 Sally — Gen 9 native review

`TRAINER_SALLY` is a four-Pokémon doubles battle in the first Trick House
puzzle. Its script uses `trainerbattle_double`; the third-badge check is for
puzzle2, not puzzle1. Route110 first access is after Brawly. Native Normal
generation at two badges/cap30 gives all four opponents level28; player
references use level30. Preserve encounter format, progression and party size.

## Accepted authoring

- Kirlia: Focus Sash → Eviolite, Timid → Modest, fast special spread →
  `252/0/0/148/108/0`. It now survives long enough to establish Calm Mind and
  exploit it. This trades Speed and one-hit Sash insurance for repeatable bulk.
- Lopunny: Facade → Fake Out. Native entry support buys its partner useful
  turns; High Jump Kick and Ice Punch retain offensive coverage. Losing
  status-boosted Facade is a real cost, not a claim of universal improvement.
- Keep Floragato and Whimsicott unchanged. Update plan/crack for the actual
  bulk/setup/pivot pattern, removing the stale Sash description and the
  implication that Fairy is super-effective against pure-Normal Lopunny.

No shared-AI changes or extra executable bonuses. Existing SETUP policy and
native move evaluation choose these actions. No wild/tutor, berry/stat-rule,
level or species-count changes.

Coaching card: Floragato applies physical pressure or switches to a suitable
partner. Kirlia takes a survivable hit, boosts if it can exploit the result,
then attacks rather than stacking indefinitely. Entering Lopunny supplies
Fake Out or immediate coverage; Scrappy prevents Ghost immunity from blanking
its Fighting/Normal moves. Whimsicott adds Fairy pressure and selective seed
support. Under Taunt or dangerous pressure, attack without insisting on setup.

Distribution: retain the sole authored Floragato and Sally's mixed trickster
identity. Later Kirlia include fast Eviolite and slow Quiet bulky versions;
Sally's specially durable Modest set is distinct. Later Lopunny include Toxic
Orb transfer, Mega, Life Orb and Normal Gem variants; this one provides Sitrus
entry support. One later Whimsicott repeats the Big Root seed set; others use
screens, Tailwind or Helping Hand. Preserve that variety rather than replacing
every support Pokémon with the same speed-control template.

## Rating

8.5/10, intention-based. More selective Encore use and cleaner reserve finishing
turns would improve Sally. She now has native setup-to-attack payoff, entry
support, typing tricks and workable reserve attacks, while physical focus,
Taunt, Poison/Steel pressure and High Jump Kick's risk remain counterplay.
