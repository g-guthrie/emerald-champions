## Encounter and accepted changes

This is her own four-Pokemon battle, not a combined neighboring encounter.
Native authored-party construction at two badges/cap30 gives all four Normal
level28, universal effective IV31 and exact authored EVs.

- **Thwackey: Miracle Seed →Eviolite; Protect →Fake Out.** Retain Adamant
  physical Speed, Grassy Surge, Grassy Glide, Knock Off and Wood Hammer.
  Improve durability and provide disruption without removing either the
  priority Grass attack or stronger recoil attack. The Grass damage bonus
  is sacrificed; Fake Out is not a promise that Quaxwell always attacks beside it.
- **Quilava: Overheat →Scorching Sands.** Keep Choice Specs, Blaze, Timid
  special Speed, Heat Wave, Flamethrower and Extrasensory. Ground coverage
  answers Fire/Electric targets and avoids the old repeated Overheat SpA
  drops under Choice lock. Specs itself still requires thoughtful switching.
- Keep Life Orb/Moxie Quaxwell and Throat Spray/Liquid Voice Brionne. No
  changed EVs, natures, species, levels or shared wild/tutor presets.
- Rewrite canonical plan/crack; retain strategy NONE and existing shared AI.
  No global policy or engine changes were required for this encounter.

A Quaxwell Eviolite trial was withdrawn. It preserved more HP in some cases
but lost knockout thresholds and therefore Moxie opportunities, and raised
the sampled decision cost to68 frames. The accepted Life Orb version keeps
the offensive engine; recoil is still real counterplay, not a defect hidden
by the review. Its main survival support is Thwackey's disruption and pressure.
