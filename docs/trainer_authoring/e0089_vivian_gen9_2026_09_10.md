`trainerbattle_double`, not a simultaneous battle with Kirk. This closes
grouped E0089 after Kirk. Actual compiled Normal/two-badge/cap30 party: six

## Team and coaching card

- Establish: keep Sitrus/Plus/Timid Plusle4HP/252SpA/252Speed. Nuzzle becomes
  Swift, giving spread neutral coverage when Electric attacks cannot work.
  Thunderbolt, Helping Hand and Protect remain. This complements Minun's
  weight-dependent coverage instead of giving both mice weak attacks into
  tiny Volt Absorb Pachirisu.
- Support: Minun keeps Minus/Timid, Thunderbolt, Light Screen and Helping Hand.
  Encore becomes Grass Knot; Light Clay becomes Sitrus, and252HP/4SpD/252Speed
  becomes252HP/164Def/92Speed. Native Defense41→53 improves physical survival;
  SpD remains72 at this level. Speed99→85 still exceeds sampled Mienfoo79,
  but no longer ties Plusle99. Helping Hand has its own priority. Screen now
  lasts five turns, not eight. This sacrifices longevity/Encore for immediate
  survival and useful coverage, not a claim of universally stronger support.
- Preserve the link: retain Eviolite/Minus/Modest Klink252HP/4Def/252SpA with
  Thunderbolt, Flash Cannon, Metal Sound and Protect. Either mouse can maintain
  the native Plus/Minus link beside it. This backup pairing is source-supported,
  not newly demonstrated in the isolated Klink/Raichu samples below.
- Establish the reserve combo: Life Orb/Lightning Rod/Timid Raichu retains
  4HP/252SpA/252Speed, Thunderbolt, Focus Blast and Protect. Nasty Plot becomes
  Fake Out to buy an immediate partner turn or stop a Ground attacker.
  Zebstrika supplies boosts without a separate Raichu setup turn. Lightning
  Rod also redirects allied single-target Electric attacks; this is not only
  protection against the player's moves.
- Exploit: Sap Sipper/Timid Zebstrika keeps4HP/252SpA/252Speed and Thunderbolt,
  Overheat, Volt Switch, Discharge. Choice Specs becomes Air Balloon: less
  special damage in exchange for Ground immunity until hit and freedom to
  change attacks after Overheat. Native Speed105 still exceeds Raichu104;
  Discharge can raise Raichu's SpA before its ordinary attack. Sap Sipper is
  defensive Grass immunity here, not an offensive Attack combo or newly
  verified activation. Volt Switch redirected into Raichu does not pivot.
- Fallback: retain Vital Spirit/Adamant Electabuzz4HP/252Atk/252Speed and
  Thunder Punch, Ice Punch, Cross Chop, Protect. Expert Belt becomes Eviolite,
  losing the super-effective damage bonus for both defenses. It can survive
  a sampled Earthquake and continue attacking. Vital Spirit execution was not
  separately demonstrated in this review.

Native damage control holds the same target/context constant and changes only
Minun's partner ability: Plusle Thunderbolt73 linked versus49 unlinked. This
is a native-calculation boundary, not two executed hits. Native Plus/Minus
support is in `battle_util.c`; Lightning Rod's absorption script raises SpA.
Accepted combo evidence uses actual move order, spent PP and resulting stages.
An earlier ordered ability-popup assertion incorrectly placed that popup after
the spread animation; removing the false ordering was a diagnostic correction,
not a gameplay repair. Other rejected diagnostics requested turns after battle
end. The dual-Grass-Knot design was changed for observed weak coverage, not to
preserve a brittle move script. No permanent per-battle test corpus was added.
