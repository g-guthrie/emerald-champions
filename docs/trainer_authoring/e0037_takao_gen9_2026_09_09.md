- Grapploct: Ice Punch to Sucker Punch. Keep Leftovers, Limber, Adamant bulky
  physical EVs, Octolock/Drain Punch/Protect. Dark priority gives the slow lead
  a concrete answer to attacking Psychic types. Giving up Ice coverage is a
  real tradeoff; Poliwrath retains it. Status moves can defeat Sucker Punch.
- Wobbuffet: Safeguard to Helping Hand. Keep Sitrus, Shadow Tag, Bold physical
  bulk, Counter/Mirror Coat/Encore. Priority support strengthens actual partner
  attacks instead of spending the opening on generic status protection.

  Fake Out/Knock Off/Blaze Kick. Close Combat consumes the Herb, restores its
  lowered defenses and doubles native Speed58 to116. Knock Off remains useful
  without requiring the item activation first.
- Poliwrath: retain Assault Vest/Water Absorb, Adamant bulky physical and
  Liquidation/Drain Punch/Ice Punch/High Horsepower. Durable Water coverage and
  partner-safe Ground coverage complement the fast Fighting reserves.
- Passimian: retain Choice Scarf/Defiant, Jolly physical Speed and Close Combat/
  Knock Off/U-turn/Rock Slide. Native58 Speed becomes87 under Scarf; Choice

## Shared Octolock correction

Native `BS_TrySetOctolock` rejects a target whose Octolock volatile is already
set, but shared bad-move scoring omitted that case. Add a zero-score rejection
for that exact condition. Fresh targets remain valid even under Shadow Tag;
Octolock's defense drops are not equivalent to Mean Look's trapping alone.
The zero score stops subsequent bonuses reviving the failed move. No recursive
forecast, new allocation or persistent state is added. This applies to all
Octolock users, not just Takao. It does not solve simultaneous duplicate casts
by two fresh-target setters or optimize future defense-drop payoff.

One retained regression calls the shared scorer used by the pair evaluator,
checks affected and fresh targets on both flanks, and checks the full pair
chooses its available attack when both foes are already affected. The original
draft incorrectly read legacy finalScore after the expert pair path; both its
before/after failures were invalid evidence. The corrected counterfactual
`work/takao-octolock-before-corrected.log` fails with score100 instead of0 when
the guard is removed. Final clean shared verification covers the restored guard.

## Native observations and boundaries

- Baseline `work/takao-gen9-baseline.log`:3 groups/17 cases. The original
  Psychic-pressure lead spends Protect/Safeguard, then Octolock/Mirror Coat;
  Grapploct falls to15HP. This proved the native Octolock cast and first drops,
  not a strong setup strategy.
- Helping Hand candidate `work/takao-gen9-support.log`:4 groups/25 cases.
  Eevee now falls on turn1; priority Helping Hand acts before opposing Fake
  Out. The original Ice Punch set still handles Psychic pressure poorly.
- Accepted Sucker Punch candidate removes Natu on turn1 with Helping Hand,
  keeping Grapploct at full HP. Drain Punch/Mirror Coat then finish Pachirisu
  on turn3 with both leads alive. The first extended fixture requested turns
  after the two-player battle had ended; its failure was corrected by stopping
  at turn3, without weakening any gameplay assertion.
- Physical lead: Helping Hand/Drain Punch removes Eevee immediately; Counter
  and supported attacks reduce Timburr to24HP by turn3. Both leads survive.
- Faster Fake Out/Taunt: Helping Hand acts first on both turns, Eevee falls
  immediately, and Mienfoo is reduced to26HP. Fast Electrike/Eevee pressure
  leaves Electrike6HP on turn2 with both leads healthy.
- Status/redirection counterplay: Tailwind plus Follow Me defeats the opening
  Sucker Punch and takes no damage. When Natu attacks on turn2, supported
  Sucker Punch removes it despite Tailwind. Drain Punch/Mirror Coat then
  reduce Pachirisu to24HP on turn3. This is recovery from a failed prediction,
  not permission for the AI to read the player's pending command.
- Middle reserve snapshot: White Herb/Unburden executes after Close Combat;
  Hitmonlee/Poliwrath finish the physical pair on turn2. Against Natu they
  instead use Knock Off/Liquidation to remove it immediately, then leave
  Pachirisu20HP on turn2. Unburden is not falsely credited to that line.
- Final reserve snapshot: Scarf Passimian removes Eevee or Natu on turn1;
  Pawmo contributes Thunder Punch/Mach Punch. Physical Timburr remains17HP
  after turn2; special Pachirisu remains43HP. These are promoted-reserve
  snapshots, not a complete six-versus-six victory or verified pivot cycle.
- Bulky Marshtomp and Timburr setup probes expose remaining prediction limits:
  Counter can fail when the physical attack instead hits Grapploct, and slow
  Encore locks Timburr's Drain Punch rather than its earlier Bulk Up. These
  acceptable tactical imperfections are not turned into more global branches.
  The final set's observed useful plan is supported trapping offense;
  reliable opportunistic Octolock payoff remains an improvement opportunity,
  not a claim established by its roster presence or synthetic regression.
