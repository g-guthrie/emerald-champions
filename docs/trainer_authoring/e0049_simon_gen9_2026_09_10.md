# E0049 Simon — Gen 9 native review

Simon is a standalone Seashore House double with `TRAINER_SIMON`; the shared
reward callback does not combine him with Dwayne or Johanna. Reviewed scope:
first-access Normal, one badge, native cap20/level18. Player references use
native level20, universal IV31, exact EVs and preparation-legal moves.

## Accepted changes

- Mudbray: Heavy Slam → Body Press. Preserve Stamina, Eviolite, Adamant,
  68HP/252Atk/188Spe, Ground/Rock coverage and Protect. Accumulated Defense
  now has an offensive outlet rather than only reducing later physical hits.
  The Attack investment still powers its STAB High Horsepower and Rock Slide.
  Losing Steel coverage is a tradeoff, not a free extra moveslot.
- Chinchou: Assault Vest → Eviolite. Preserve Water Absorb, Modest,
  252HP/4Def/252SpA and all four attacking moves. As an unevolved Pokémon it
  keeps special durability while gaining physical bulk, improving the weak
  reserve reference without changing offense or Speed.
- Prinplup and Morelull remain unchanged; no species, other moves, abilities,
  EVs, level offsets, stat rules or shared-AI changes. Updated prose describes
  the existing evaluator's options, not a new executable script.

All508-EV spreads and universal IV31/berry rules stay exact. Prinplup39
precedes Mudbray35; an Icy Wind changes Pachirisu49→32, but Natu56→37 still
outruns Mudbray. No blanket claim that one speed drop wins every matchup.
Chinchou34 is slower than ordinary opposing attackers, useful for an optional
slow pivot, but Volt Switch is not demonstrated in this batch.

Native Body Press selects Defense and its relevant stage as its attacking
stat; the pair evaluator has an explicit corresponding modifier. Eviolite
improves durability, not an extra direct Body Press damage multiplier. At +2
Defense Mudbray has70 before other modifiers versus its ordinary62 Attack,
and Fighting coverage offers different matchups. This is theme3's defensive/
offensive Stamina building block, not an allied multihit activation engine.

Distribution: retain one of two early Prinplup and Mudbray slots, the only
authored Morelull and the third Chinchou. Morelull keeps a distinct Grass/Fairy
sleep-support role; Chinchou's Water Absorb differs from earlier Volt Absorb
versions. No wild/tutor data, species counts or habitats changed.

Coaching card: attack directly when possible, or use Icy Wind for a real
crossing. Punish opposing drops through Competitive. Survive incoming hits
with Stamina and exploit either Body Press or the more suitable Ground/Rock
attack. Morelull supplies Spore and spread Fairy pressure; Chinchou supplies
special offense and an optional pivot. Strength Sap remains conditional
recovery, with the observed decision weakness explicitly retained below.

## Native evidence

`work/simon-gen9-final.log`:5 groups/38 parameter cases PASS. Actual compiled
party generation supplies opponents. Promoted reserve cases explicitly start
with Morelull/Chinchou; they are not proof that every full battle reaches that
pair. Native lead switches and replacements are unforced. These references
are not measured win rates; observed secondary-effect rolls are not guaranteed.

- Physical lead: Protect/High Horsepower, then Hydro Pump/Body Press removes
  Eevee. Stamina accumulates from incoming Drain Punch; continued attacks
  finish Timburr turn4, Prinplup56HP/Mudbray19HP. Heavy Slam's original line
  had similar immediate outcome, but Body Press now expresses the Defense
  engine rather than adding an arbitrary setup reward.
- Concentrated hits into Mudbray: it survives two turn1 attacks at20HP and
  +2 Defense, asserted. Turn2 actual Body Press is asserted by its native
  animation; Mudbray survives subsequent hits at10 and3HP while attacking.
  Both foes fall by turn4, Prinplup full56HP/Mudbray3HP. Ground remains the
  preferred attack into the remaining Fighting target rather than forcing
  Body Press every turn just because Defense is boosted.
- Psychic/Electric lead: Rock Slide pressures both foes while Prinplup
  protects, then the native AI switches Prinplup to Chinchou. Natu falls
  turn2; Muddy Water/High Horsepower finish Pachirisu turn3, Chinchou39HP/
  Mudbray7HP. Original Heavy Slam left Pachirisu19HP at that point.
- Opposing Icy Wind: Competitive gives Prinplup +2 SpA, asserted; Mudbray
  receives a Stamina boost from the damaging special hit too. High Horsepower
  removes Pachirisu turn1. Body Press, then Icy Wind/Rock Slide, and finally
  Ice Beam/Rock Slide finish Lotad turn4, Prinplup52HP/Mudbray49HP. This
  preserves coverage and changes move choice according to the target rather
  than insisting on one STAB attack or unnecessary repeated speed control.
- Fake Out/Taunt lead: Prinplup protects, Mudbray attacks into Mienfoo, then
  Prinplup switches to Morelull. Fairy/Ground attacks finish both foes turn4,
  Morelull59HP/Mudbray61HP. Electric attacks into Mudbray do no damage; they

- Promoted physical reserves: Eviolite Chinchou survives at43HP on turn1

  pressure, not a claimed completed win.
- Promoted Psychic/Electric reserves: Thunderbolt damages/paralyzes Natu,
  then attacks remove it turn2. Morelull uses Spore on Pachirisu turn3

- Reserve Fake Out/Taunt: Fake Out interrupts Morelull first, then Taunt

  Strength Sap is not selected despite low HP; Morelull is extremely slow,
  but this still leaves recovery decision quality unproven. Do not advertise
  a demonstrated healing loop or solve it by forcing Sap in every low-HP state.

The native evidence does not demonstrate Volt Switch, Water Absorb healing,
or Strength Sap. Source availability is not execution proof. Ordinary useful
sleep, Stamina/Body Press and Competitive are demonstrated; the optional
recovery/pivot refinements remain follow-up work under diminishing returns.
