# Gen 9 EV migration and source ownership

**Latest berry override:** [universal cutoff +1HP](berry_thresholds_2026_09_09.md).
The earlier parity search, ±40 tolerance and two normalization checks described
below are superseded and deleted. Preset application and Leveler use now preserve
exact authored/custom EVs. The Gen9 formula and perfect-IV policy are unchanged.

Implemented September 9, 2026, following the user's approval of conventional
EVs, universal perfect IVs and a single-source refactor. This supersedes both
the flat Stat Points system and the intermediate level-scaled 66/32 system.

## Live owners

- `include/constants/pokemon_stats.h`: IV31 and EV252/stat, 510/total limits.
  Authoring tools read this same header through `scripts/emerald_champions_evs.py`.
- `src/pokemon.c:CalculateSpeciesStat`: the sole stat formula. Both ordinary
  recalculation and Battle Dome estimates call it. Nature is applied after
  level scaling; Shedinja remains 1 HP. Normal recalculation preserves fainting.
- `data/emerald_champions/emerald_champions_hand_audited_battle_sets.json`:
  the sole editable shared preset catalog, including ordering and named aliases.
  Tutor, ordinary wild and evolution defaults use its generated tables.
- `data/emerald_champions/emerald_champions_battle_teams.txt`: authored campaign
  trainer teams and tactics. Master team/design sections, plans and trainer
  parties are outputs; no reverse-seeding route remains. Other master encounter
  metadata remains an independent input, not duplicated team ownership.
- Circuit's distinct pinned upstream templates remain its own source for its
  generated teams. Its 403 shared authored presets are now species/name
  references into the canonical catalog, never independently editable copies.
- Four identical gift presets reference shared defaults. Three genuinely
  distinct gifts and the bespoke opening loadouts retain their own definitions.
  Unifying them with different defaults would change gameplay, not deduplicate it.

The preset generator no longer constructs an obsolete archive/handbook/Showdown
catalog and then overwrites it. Its former 2,561 lines are reduced to about 400.
The unused divergent backward draft was preserved under `docs/archive/`, marked
historical and not a generator input. No distinct draft loadouts were destroyed.

## Stat and allocation behavior

Non-HP: floor((2*base + 31 + floor(EV/4))*level/100) + 5, then Nature.
HP: floor((2*base + 31 + floor(EV/4))*level/100) + level + 10.
There is no additional progression curve or automatic level-50 battle setting.
Campaign caps/difficulty remain unchanged. Above-100 Circuit recalculation keeps
its existing battle-only level handling.

Both packed and individual IV writes store 31. There is no IV editor, random IV
roll or gradual IV growth. Consequently zero-Speed/zero-Attack IV builds are not
options, and ordinary IV-based Hidden Power resolves to Dark. Existing custom
moves, abilities and species are retained.

The one-time conversion mapped positive old points P to 8P-4 EVs, then removed
overflow above 508 in increments of four from the smallest positive allocation
(display-order ties). Example: 32/32/2 becomes 252/252/4. The conversion is done;
live authoring accepts EVs directly and must never reconvert them. 6,923 shared
presets and 2,339 members across 516 trainer branches were converted. Their
non-stat loadouts were preserved, including previously pending authored edits.

This is a fresh-save build, following the project's explicit no-legacy-save
migration policy. Old saves containing 0–32 Stat Points are not automatically
converted to EVs by loading them; existing allocation bytes would mean EVs.

The tutor offers +/-4, 16 and 32 EVs, zero, maximum and full reset, with live
native stats and atomic per-stat/total clamps. This adapts Inclement Emerald's
actual Super Training cap/reset helpers, not a claim to copy its entire UI:
https://github.com/jrci-dev/inclementemerald/blob/93e96730fb3c771f7f08f9623ae36f0643c52d3d/src/field_specials.c#L5152
Passive battle EV gain remains disabled so played battles do not drift an
edited spread. Berry parity adjustment uses bounded four-EV steps within 40 EVs
of authored HP and the same recognition bound at subsequent level caps. The
September 9 follow-up widened the former 24-EV bound because level-14 Snorlax
needs a 28-EV reduction; Sitrus remains exempt and keeps ceil-half activation.

### September 9 follow-up: Protect experiment withdrawn

Removing the scalar first-use Protect bonus did not resolve the actual E0001
double guards and changed an unrelated synthetic recoil/berry decision. That
unaccepted experiment was withdrawn; no expected move was changed to mask its
failure. The counterfactual's incomplete turn was a missing replacement record
for opponentLeft (Treecko), not a runtime freeze. The scratch recording now
supplies that replacement and logs party-slot HP rather than mislabeling the
replacement's HP as Treecko's. The runner's incomplete-turn message now identifies
the battler and requested record type/index.

With current Gen 9 stats and deterministic hits/no critical hits, forced
Protect/Thunderbolt leaves Treecko35HP, Pikachu3HP and opposing Mienfoo7/41HP;
forced Seed Bomb/Electroweb loses Treecko, leaves Pikachu3HP and foes20/40HP
at -1 Speed, with Taillow replacing Treecko. Thus the formerly useful attack
line is not the same bargain under the new stats. These are specified-action
counterfactuals, not unrestricted AI wins or justification for reading pending
player commands. All 50 selected `EC ` groups (46 retained + four temporary
E0001 groups) pass after withdrawal; E0001 remains OPEN. Log:
`work/e1-protect-experiment-withdrawn.log`.
