# Emerald Champions player context

You are playing Emerald Champions through screenshots and ordinary GBA button
presses. Your objective is to finish the whole game under the configured action,
frame, death, and time budgets. Explore, read dialogue, use native saves, and
complete tutorials and story interactions; do not assume content is optional
just because it is not a battle.

## Rules you should plan around

- Campaign level caps are strict. This benchmark always uses the authored Hard
  opponent levels and AI.
- Most trainer encounters are Doubles, though intentional Singles and multi
  battles remain. Wild encounters are ordinary battles. Build for partner
  synergy, positioning, speed control, protection, disruption, and focus fire,
  while retaining workable Singles options.
- Mega Evolution is the only selectable gimmick. Do not plan around Z-Moves,
  Dynamax, Terastallization, or other later-generation gimmicks. Approved
  Groudon and Kyogre Primal Reversion is automatic and species-specific.
- The Inclement Emerald story and world remain intact. Exploration, NPCs,
  routes, puzzles, native captures, and progression gates matter.

## Preparation resources

Pokémon Centers are your preparation hub. Return there whenever a loss or a new
challenge shows that the current plan is inadequate.

For a battle-lab run you receive a checkpoint-bound legal arsenal generated
from source reachability. It includes all currently obtainable route, gift,
static, and starter species, the active cap, and only currently legal items and
stones. You may request legal preparation directly instead of spending the
benchmark on menu dexterity; inaccessible resources must be rejected. The
request does not choose strategy for you. Each independent battle puzzle gives
you the full generated opponent dossier, including its exact team; use it to
construct and pilot a legal answer. Navigation, scouting, and grinding are
outside the score.

- The reusable Leveler is available from the first Pokémon Center and raises
  the party to the current cap without grinding. Rare Candy is not stocked by
  the active marts; use the Leveler.
- The move specialist offers every supported legal move for the species,
  including legal level, pre-evolution, egg, TM, tutor, inherited, reviewed
  event, and explicit Inclement-extension sources.
- The build selector offers curated Singles and Doubles presets that set moves,
  nature, Ability, held item, and Stat Points together. Many species have two to
  four Doubles choices plus Singles choices; some have more. Inspect alternatives
  instead of assuming the first preset is best.
- The party menu can select a Pokémon's legal Abilities. The Stat Point editor
  can redistribute exactly 66 Champions Stat Points, with at most 32 in one stat.
- Perfect-IV backing is fixed. Breeding and money grinding should not be needed
  to produce a competitive team.
- Ordinary competitive held items are broadly available from the native Center
  vendor. Berries, Mega Stones, evolution and transformation items retain their
  own progression rules. Mega Stone and evolution-item archives open after
  Steven gives the Mega Ring following Brawly.
- The Poké Vial restores the party outside battle and gains finite capacity
  through its quest. Use Centers and available healing resources deliberately.

## Resourceful retry policy

Do not repeat a failed battle indefinitely with the same plan. After a loss,
state what failed and choose a concrete adaptation. Depending on evidence, you
may catch different Pokémon, level the party to the current cap, reorder or
replace party members, change moves or a Singles/Doubles preset, switch held
items or legal Abilities, redistribute Stat Points, revisit a Center or vendor,
and then retry. Record the adaptation rationale and the battle attempt so the
benchmark can distinguish reasonable difficulty from blind repetition.

You receive the opponent dossier, but not a bespoke counter-team or written
solution. Team building,
battle choices, loss diagnosis, adaptation, and retries remain your work even
when routine navigation to a legitimate checkpoint is assisted. Preserve resources when possible, use
native saving at sensible milestones, and explore for routes and services when
progress is unclear.
