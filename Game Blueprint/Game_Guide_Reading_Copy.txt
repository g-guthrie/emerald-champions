EMERALD CHAMPIONS — GAME GUIDE, V4
================================

This book explains the game we are making. It owns the intended experience, story and design choices. Its short guide is followed by a generated reference containing exact teams, world data and source dialogue. Source files own those records; the reference is rebuilt and checked automatically. Test logs and development diaries stay outside the book. When a design changes, update this guide only where the experience changes and update the corresponding source at the same time.

Current focus: comprehensively audit every retained trainer in first-access order, starting after Rustboro Gym. The Cut-enclosure block and the Rusturf-to-Dewford team designs are implemented; the seven Brawly Gym designs, seven first-arrival beach/Seashore House designs and the reviewed blocks through Flannery are implemented:131 first-access encounters, including optional summit/Lavaridge battles and the early Ashen Woods detour. The retained roster is341 encounters; Gina and Mia remain an explicit keep. The current task is the question-led design audit, with each trainer discussed in chat; full battle benchmarks and exhaustive AI validation are separate later work and do not block the next design group. The later earned C15 playthrough is a separate, paused development run; its save and unfinished Dwayne experiment are described in docs/CONTINUE.md. Reviewed teams are not automatically difficulty-tested. Later chapters below describe the intended campaign; native play and scene acceptance are unfinished.

1. THE EXPERIENCE
-----------------

Emerald Champions is a difficult competitive doubles adventure built on the native Emerald/Inclement/Expansion foundation. Battles should be memorable puzzles with strong Pokémon, useful support partners, competent AI and several lines of play. The player should improve at competitive decisions rather than spend hours fixing stats, acquiring ordinary equipment or repeating a grind.

The most important question after building or reviewing EVERY team is: “Could this team be cooler?” Ask it again after your own redesign. Judge the partnership, Pokémon choices, regional identity, alternate lines and counterplay. A legal team, a rare species or six filled slots is not enough. Keep a good low-stat or baby theme and adjust individual levels when strength is the problem. Do not evolve everything, replace unusual support with generic attackers or weaken correct AI just to make a fight easier.

Use a broad, coherent roster across generations. Preserve signature habitats and early useful choices. All supported Mega types should have meaningful opportunities, but a name on a checklist is not an executed showcase. Do not add battles or wild slots to satisfy quotas. Pokémon gifts, natural habitats, legendary scenes and opponent teams have different jobs; one does not substitute for the others.

Every retained campaign trainer uses doubles or a deliberately authored multi battle. Ordinary trainer teams generally have at least four members; every Gym trainer and leader has six. The player chooses two starters from one selected region and the rival uses the unchosen third. Ordinary wild and legendary captures remain singles. The opening Birch rescue is a bespoke low-level double before normal preparation.

2. RULES, PREPARATION AND PROGRESSION
-----------------------------------

The first Oldale visit, before the prepared Route103 rival, provides healing, storage, the Leveler, legal moves and build editing. Nature, ability, EVs, useful IVs, friendship and ordinary battle equipment are free preparation choices. Maximum PP is fixed at its fully improved value. A preset is a convenience, not a different legality system or permission to manufacture progression items. All ordinary battle berries are freely available for equipping and planting; they cannot become free manual medicine or resale profit.

Manual medicine and X-items cannot brute-force trainer or wild fights. Poké Balls remain usable for catches, and held effects and healing/revival moves remain ordinary battle mechanics. Battle-start held items, including consumed berries, are restored at settlement. Field Potions, Revives, status cures and PP recovery remain exploration conveniences. Centers, the refillable Vial and established rest points reduce the need to purchase them.

Easy, Medium and Hard adjust enemy levels only, by −2/0/+2. Medium is the new-game default. They do not change teams, AI, story or the player cap. Each trainer member uses the live player cap plus its authored individual offset and the difficulty adjustment. Optional return fights scale with the live cap; old absolute levels in generated trainer records are previews. Opponents can exceed100 within the native1–255 representation. No hidden stat multiplier replaces level tuning.

Player caps rise after milestones, not during their boss fights. Captures, gifts, evolution and preparation respect the current individual cap. Legendary/mythical species above600 configured BST use floor(chapter cap ×600/BST), with a minimum of1. Species at or below600 retain the chapter cap. Ultra Beasts/Paradox species are not automatically restricted. Temporary Mega/Primal forms do not incur a second reduction. No additional Mega-ready penalty is approved.

3. THE CAMPAIGN
---------------

This is one connected adventure: two partners and the Gym journey lead into stolen survey equipment, the old land/sea accounts, the teams' misuse of those discoveries, and the eventual weather crisis. Required exploration must have a clear local reason and an explicit next destination. Optional captures are not story keys unless a chapter explicitly requires a species. A report means a consequential conversation, not repeated generic errands.

C01 — Two partners. Choose both starters and rescue Birch. His encouragement begins the journey. Go north through Route101 to Oldale before meeting the rival.

C03 — First-arrival Oldale. Heal and prepare before the first real trainer test. Services and basic catching supplies are already available; the chapter number is not an access lock. Go north to Route103.

C02 — Rival and send-off. Battle the unchosen starter and its partners, then return to Birch's lab for the Pokédex and supplies. A pending gift cannot block send-off. Travel west from Oldale through Route102 toward Norman.

C04 — Norman and Wally. Meet Norman and help Wally begin. Establish the personal relationship that will return at Mauville and Victory Road. Norman directs the player west and north through Petalburg Woods to Roxanne.

C05 — Woods ambush. Defend the Devon employee and his survey records. Keep the existing approach scene and habitats. His gratitude and onward journey motivate the later Rustboro theft; send the player north rather than back to another report.

C06 — Rustboro. Explore specialists, obtain the Cut license and optionally trade for Fidough or buy evolutions. The Gym remains the immediate objective. Cut becomes usable after the badge.

C07 — Roxanne. Four distinct six-member rooms culminate in Mega Aerodactyl-led fossil offense. Josh has living minerals; Tommy a fossil reef; Marc prehistoric Power Spot support. Roxanne uses Tailwind, not Trick Room. She explains Mega Evolution and awards Old Amber with Devon revival directions. Earn cap20, then meet the employee about the theft.

C08 — Rusturf rescue. Follow Route116 east, confront the thug, recover the goods and rescue Peeko. Briney's gratitude establishes future transport. Return west to the Devon employee.

C09 — Devon. Mr. Stone connects Steven's letter and Stern's survey equipment. Deliveries become the next journey through Dewford to Slateport. Fossil revival is a free optional service, not a required extra report.

C10 — Briney's passage. Sail from the existing Route104 cottage to Dewford. Preserve the boarding choreography and the captain's relationship to Peeko.

C11 — Dewford discovery. Town, meadow and manor conversation links expedition records to Steven. Orient the player toward Granite Cave, Brawly and the meadow; fishing and trades are optional. Explain where to obtain a Sing-capable Pokémon before the musical scene.

C12 — Letter to Steven. Deliver the letter in Granite Cave. The hiker's Flash license supports this first journey. Steven connects geology with the manor's accounts and directs the investigation onward.

C13 — Brawly and bracelet. Beat Brawly, then return to Steven in Granite Cave; Brawly repeats these exact directions. Steven gives the bracelet and Aerodactylite, with independent supported-starter stone entitlements. Each chosen starter's receipt is separate; unsupported Mega lines receive no invented stone. The Ring does not require owning Aerodactyl or completing fossil revival.

C14 — Manor and song. Investigate the manor and perform the meadow song with the indicated local resource. Witness the Meloetta discovery; ownership is optional. The account introduces the crystal mystery beneath Sootopolis and leads back to Stern's observations. Finish at cap24 and sail to Slateport.

C15 — Slateport. The market offers optional preparation and evolution choices. Find Stern at the shipyard/museum. No shopping tutorial or purchase threshold blocks the mission.

C16 — Museum conflict. Deliver the goods and stop Aqua's attempt to seize the marine survey equipment. Their underwater search becomes clear. Keep the retained grunt and Archie; the redundant middle fight stays retired. Reach cap30 and travel inland.

C17 — Route110 rival. The rival notices the investigation but tests the player as a trainer. Keep that personal competition alongside the larger story. Continue north to Mauville.

C18 — Wally and Mauville. Resolve Wally's challenge, obtain Rock Smash and bike access, and prepare for Wattson. Game Corner purchases are optional, never purchased story clues.

C19 — Wattson. Win the Dynamo Badge and cap34. Establish Wattson's power expertise for the later generator problem. Rock Smash opens the next route.

C20 — Tunnel reunion. Visit Verdanturf and reunite the couple through Rusturf, paying off the earlier blocked passage. Receive Strength, usable after Flannery. Day Care, breeding and local gifts remain optional.

C21 — Ash country. Follow the northern route toward Fallarbor and Meteor Falls. Existing residents explain the volcano and team activity. Soot collection is an optional side activity, never the reason the main story waits.

C22 — Meteor Falls and summit. Discover the meteorite theft and stop Magma at Mt. Chimney. Modern equipment and old accounts now serve the same dangerous search. Preserve the authored multi battle and retained summit opponents; no duplicate grunt leg. Resolve the local disturbance and reach cap40.

C23 — Flannery. Defeat her in Lavaridge, reach cap44 and receive the established onward access. Her local perspective leads toward trouble in Ashen Woods. Wynaut and other optional gifts retain their identity.

C24 — Ashen rescue. The caretaker is the existing Route111 nurse beside the woods. Preserve her position and the Chansey chase. Her repeat dialogue points to Moltres and, afterward, Sandstrewn Ruins.

C27 — Norman. Defeat him, earn Surf from Wally's family and reach cap50. This is both a personal payoff and access east. Wattson now needs help at New Mauville across Route110's water.

C28 — New Mauville. Resolve the restarting generator and Rotom encounter. Wattson sends the readings to the Institute himself; the player is already traveling that way. Conversation/completion earns cap52 and research access, independently of pending Electirizer delivery or Rotom capture.

C35 — Southern Island. The sanctuary is the first offshore stop. Resolve the Lati scene and continue directly to Faraway using the earned chart. Ownership is optional; the scene shows a living habitat, not a weapon collection.

C36 — Faraway. Follow Mew's existing chase, resolve the encounter and return with Briney. Reach cap64. The old account now points to the Orbs' custodians at Mt. Pyre; do not send the player through a generic report counter.

C37 — Orb theft. Witness the teams acting on the same history. The elders explain why the Orbs are dangerous as supposed control devices. Pursue Magma; optional additional legends do not delay the chase.

C41 — Tate and Liza. Earn the Mind Badge in a major coordinated doubles battle. Steven will supply Dive after the nearby Space Center event. The next threat is local and immediate.

Room entry, capture, Magearna ownership and exhibitions are not substitutes or gates. Record observation before an optional battle can remove the actor. Reach cap94 and finish the research thread before the League.

C49 — Victory Road Wally. His confidence pays off Petalburg and Mauville. Defeat him, reach cap96 and provide the final outside preparation opportunity.
