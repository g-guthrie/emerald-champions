INCLEMENT EMERALD 2 - WHAT EACH BATTLE'S CHALLENGER CAN HAVE, ROUTE BY ROUTE
Written by hand, route by route, with every entry checked in source and approved by the owner.

Rules
  A route trainer's challenger has every area walked before that route, never the route's own new
  Pokemon or items. Exception: the first rival battle on Route 103 also counts Route 103.
  Gym, League and Champion battles count everything reachable when the player walks in.
  Starters: one region, two of its three (Game Corner opens the rest later).
  Levels: the Leveler raises the party to the cap. Evolutions listed are only those reachable by
  the step's cap. Friendship evolutions use the Center tutor's Bonding (data/scripts/emerald_champions.inc).
  EVs: none, player or trainer, before the Knuckle Badge (src/caps.c AreEVsUnlocked).
  Old Rod: from Mom at the start (data/maps/LittlerootTown/scripts.inc:952); fishing slots 0-1
  (src/wild_encounter.c:342). Surf tables wait for Surf.
  Shops: every Poke Mart at 0 badges sells Mental Herb, Red Card, Cell Battery
  (data/scripts/poke_mart.inc:19). The Center vendor's first-talk kit is Choice Band, Choice Specs,
  Choice Scarf, Focus Sash, Eviolite, Leftovers (src/field_specials.c:606); buying, picking up,
  receiving or catching a Pokemon holding a catalogue item adds it to the vendor shelf for good,
  and catching a species adds its signature item (src/field_specials.c:485-506).
  Wild held items: listed per step as (common/rare) from the species' itemCommon/itemRare.
