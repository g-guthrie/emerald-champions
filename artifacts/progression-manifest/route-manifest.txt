INCLEMENT EMERALD 2 - WHAT EACH BATTLE'S CHALLENGER CAN HAVE, ROUTE BY ROUTE
Written by hand, route by route, with every entry checked in source and approved by the owner.

Rules
  A route trainer's challenger has every area walked before that route, never the route's own new
  Pokemon or items. Exception: the first rival battle on Route 103 also counts Route 103.
  Gym, League and Champion battles count everything reachable when the player walks in.
  Starters: one region, two of its three (Game Corner opens the rest later).
  Levels: the Leveler raises the party to the cap. Evolutions listed are only those reachable by
  the step's cap. Friendship evolutions use the Center tutor's Bonding (data/scripts/emerald_champions.inc).
  EVs: none, player or trainer, before the Knuckle Badge (src/caps.c AreEVsUnlocked).
  Old Rod: from Mom at the start (data/maps/LittlerootTown/scripts.inc:952); fishing slots 0-1
  (src/wild_encounter.c:342). Surf tables wait for Surf.
  Shops: every Poke Mart at 0 badges sells Mental Herb, Red Card, Cell Battery
  (data/scripts/poke_mart.inc:19). The Center vendor's first-talk kit is Choice Band, Choice Specs,
  Choice Scarf, Focus Sash, Eviolite, Leftovers (src/field_specials.c:606); buying, picking up,
  receiving or catching a Pokemon holding a catalogue item adds it to the vendor shelf for good,
  and catching a species adds its signature item (src/field_specials.c:485-506).
  Wild held items: listed per step as (common/rare) from the species' itemCommon/itemRare.

STEP 1 - cap 14 - Rival May / Brendan, Route 103 (battle #1, all six starter variants)
  Pokemon
    Starters (Littleroot): any region's pair.
    Route 101 grass: Fidough, Hoppip, Lillipup, Pawmi, Poochyena, Scatterbug (Icy Snow), Wurmple,
      Zigzagoon, Zorua
    Route 103 grass: Blitzle, Grubbin, Kricketot, Rockruff, Shellos (West), Shinx, Snubbull, Toxel,
      Wingull
    Evolutions by 14: Silcoon/Cascoon -> Beautifly/Dustox, Kricketune, Spewpa -> Vivillon (Icy Snow);
      a Chimchar or Cyndaquil starter reaches Monferno or Quilava.
  Battle items
    Center vendor kit: Choice Band, Choice Specs, Choice Scarf, Focus Sash, Eviolite, Leftovers
    Poke Mart: Mental Herb, Red Card, Cell Battery
    Wild held: Wurmple (Pecha Berry / Bright Powder), Zigzagoon (Potion / Revive), Kricketot (- / Metronome),
      Wingull (Pretty Feather / -)

STEP 3 - cap 14 - Route 104 south trainers (Billy, Darian, Cindy)
  Adds Route 102 and Petalburg City.
  Pokemon
    Route 102 grass: Toedscool, Lotad, Seedot, Happiny, Bidoof, Tympole, Gothita, Surskit, Nymble,
      Lechonk, Ralts, Meowth
    Old Rod: Route 102 Corphish, Goldeen; Petalburg Magikarp, Marill
    Honey (Honey from wild Surskit or Combee): Combee (Routes 101, 102), Cutiefly (Route 103);
      Route 102's honey table also has Ralts, Gothita, Bidoof
    Evolutions by 14: Lombre (Lotad 14), Nuzleaf (Seedot 14). Happiny needs an Oval Stone (none yet).
    Not yet: Petalburg's Abomasite and Venusaurite (pond islands, need Surf); Surf tables.
  Battle items
    Berry trees (Route 102): Oran, Pecha
    Wild held: Lotad (- / Mental Herb), Seedot (- / Power Herb), Meowth (- / Quick Claw),
      Goldeen (- / Mystic Water), Surskit (- / Honey), Combee (Honey / -)
  Not here: Chilan Berry (Petalburg Gym guide, after the fourth badge); Lucky Punch (needs Chansey
  caught).
