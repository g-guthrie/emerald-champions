INCLEMENT EMERALD 2 - WHAT EACH BATTLE'S CHALLENGER CAN HAVE, ROUTE BY ROUTE
Written route by route from play (artifacts/playthrough: SWEEP.md, EVIDENCE.md, NOTES.md) and checked
in source; every step is approved by the owner before the next.

Rules
  Battle numbers (E0001...) are the encounter IDs of data/emerald_champions/emerald_champions_battle_teams.txt,
  in story order; retired numbers (data/emerald_champions/retired_battles.json) are skipped. Every
  live battle belongs to exactly one step; the trainer audit walks the steps in order.
  A route trainer's challenger has every area walked before that route, never the route's own new
  Pokemon or items. Exception: the first rival battle on Route 103 also counts Route 103.
  Gym, League and Champion battles count everything reachable when the player walks in.
  Starters: one region, two of its three (Game Corner opens the rest later).
  Levels: the Leveler raises the party to the cap. Evolutions listed are only those reachable by
  the step's cap. No friendship evolution happens before the Stone Badge (owner decision;
  src/pokemon.c IF_MIN_FRIENDSHIP checks FLAG_BADGE01_GET; the rival says so with the Cherish
  Balls). Friendship still builds by walking, and the Center tutor's Bonding opens with the same
  Badge, so a raised Budew or Togepi evolves on its first level-up after it.
  EVs: none, player or trainer, before the Knuckle Badge (src/caps.c AreEVsUnlocked).
  Wild Pokemon: the in-game DexNav (Start menu) photographed on every area, checked against
  src/data/wild_encounters.json. It lists only slots that can start a battle now (no Surf table
  without Surf, no Honey table without Honey).
  Old Rod: from Mom after the Pokedex (VAR_LITTLEROOT_TOWN_STATE 3); fishing slots 0-1
  (src/wild_encounter.c:342). Surf tables wait for Surf.
  Shops: every Poke Mart at 0 badges sells Mental Herb, Red Card, Cell Battery
  (data/scripts/poke_mart.inc:19). The Center clerk's first-talk kit is Choice Band, Choice Specs,
  Choice Scarf, Focus Sash, Eviolite, Leftovers (src/field_specials.c GiveEmeraldChampionsStarter-
  BattleItems); a pickup, gift, purchase or catch of a holder adds a catalogue item to the clerk's
  Held Items shelves for good, and badge floors stock the rest (sBadgeStockedBattleItems).
  Wild held items: battle items held by wild Pokemon met before the first Badge were moved to the
  clerk at floor 0 (owner decision): Bright Powder, Mental Herb, Metronome, Mystic Water, Power
  Herb, Quick Claw. Berries, Honey, medicine and sell items stay on wild Pokemon. Listed per step
  from the species' itemCommon/itemRare.
  Mega Stones: none before the Mega Ring (Wattson, Dynamo Badge; owner decision). Early gifts give
  a battle item instead and the stone waits in a sparkle behind Rock Smash (licensed with the same
  Badge): Glimmoranite (Granite B1F) for Roxanne's Rocky Helmet, Machampite (Rusturf Tunnel) for
  Brawly's Punching Glove, Aggronite (Granite B2F) for the Devon employee's Eject Pack.
  Worthless pickups (Potion, Ether, Repel, Escape Rope, status heals) become battle items.
