# Emerald Champions Physical One-Off Encounter Visual Audit

## Scope and source of truth

This is the source-of-truth ledger for the **32 physical one-off Pokémon
encounter objects** compiled from live `data/maps/*/map.json`. The literal map
scan finds 38 `OBJ_EVENT_GFX_SPECIES(...)` objects. Five Poké Vial Chansey
service mascots and the non-catchable Cave of Origin Carbink story gate are
excluded, leaving exactly 32 encounter objects.

This revision re-read every live map object and re-inspected all 32 current
`encounter-##-species.png` frames plus the same-camera
`pecharunt-shrine-background.png` proof. No design document supplied placement
data. Each row reads:

- the current map JSON object and exact object index, coordinates, elevation,
  script, and hide/caught flag;
- the referenced layout's current `map.bin` for occupied and cardinal tile
  collision/elevation;
- the live species overworld declaration, frame table, and source PNG;
- every object or background/sign event within Chebyshev distance two;
- the current deterministic actual-map screenshot and its visual verdict.

`c0` is collision 0 and `c1` is collision 1. `eN` is the four-bit tile
elevation. “Object e3” is the map-template elevation; the engine's
`ObjectEventUpdateElevation` synchronizes an active object to its live tile
elevation. Cardinal entries always show all four adjacent tiles, including
blocked or out-of-bounds tiles.

Camera risk is reproducible: a 15x10 GBA viewport cannot fully center an object
toward an edge when its margin is below seven horizontal or five vertical
tiles. This is a source framing-review flag, not a visual failure by itself.
“Nearby” means another object/sign within two tiles and is likewise a review
signal, not proof that both flags can be active simultaneously.

A screenshot is **CLEAN** only when the frame is the actual map, the complete
Pokémon and player are visible, the palette and scale are coherent, and neither
map geometry nor nearby objects/signs obscure the subject. **INCONCLUSIVE**
means the frame cannot approve composition even if the source geometry is
otherwise valid.

## Static and rendered findings

- **32/32** objects are in bounds and resolve to a source sprite sheet/frame
  table.
- **29** occupied tiles are collision 0; **3** are collision 1:
  MELOETTA, PECHARUNT, RESHIRAM.
- **32/32** have at least one adjacent collision-0 approach tile.
- **6** template/tile elevations differ: MELOETTA, DIALGA, CRESSELIA, PECHARUNT, RESHIRAM, ARTICUNO. Runtime
  elevation synchronization means these are presentation/approach reviews, not
  static proof of inaccessibility.
- **9** objects have another object or sign within two tiles.
- **16** objects are camera-edge-constrained under the stated 15x10
  criterion; **0** now originate on an outermost map tile.
- **6** use 64x64 frames: DIALGA, ZYGARDE, RESHIRAM, REGIGIGAS, PALKIA, ENAMORUS.
- Production moves are reflected live: Magearna **(8,7)**, Fezandipiti
  **(13,12)**, and Virizion **(42,6)**.
- **32/32** encounter frames plus **one** supplemental same-camera Pecharunt
  background frame are visually clean and runtime-verified in the authoritative
  92-scenario `manifest.json` against fixture ROM SHA-256
  `efa3292fab99bdfcdb8746976f676e68fef7fbd7c109426d8ecc3ac66b626e44`.
- Reshiram's final frame is the post-message 16:15:26 recenter with player at
  **(10,5)**; the complete sprite is visible and approved.
