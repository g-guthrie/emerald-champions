Sweet Scent ordinary rarity reversal — September 10, 2026

Sweet Scent now reverses the combined normal encounter probabilities of ordinary species in its active land or water table. Duplicate slots are aggregated before sorting; reversed weights are shared equally among species tied at the same original rarity. Thus 60/18/12/10 becomes 10/12/18/60, and 36/36/28 becomes 32/32/36. Duplicate slots still retain their original within-species level distribution.

The native/quest legendary overlay remains a separate shared 25% roll among eligible, uncaught species. When it does not produce a legendary, the ordinary result uses the reversed species table. Ordinary-table legends (Ultra Beasts) are excluded from the rarity reversal and retain their normal slot probability while uncaught; their caught slots feed the ordinary reversed pool. Capture requirements and permanent caught flags are unchanged. Ordinary walking and lure selection keep their existing implementation; Sweet Scent uses its own chooser, independently of type-attracting lead abilities. The temporary scent state is cleared after both successful and failed field use. Fishing and Rock Smash are not triggered by Sweet Scent.

Route rosters and the early Center Repel Spray tutorial explain the effect. The route explanation is informational and does not add a menu.
